- **Paged terrain uploads moved to the shader pack.** `PagedTerrain`'s upload journal (`updates`, `bindings`, `isUploaded`, `initialTextures`, `uploadJournal`) and `awake:terrain`'s `TERRAIN_HEIGHT_PAGES_BINDING` and `TERRAIN_PAGE_TABLE_BINDING` are deprecated. Use `PagedTerrainUploads` and the same-named constants in `awake:asset:shader-pack`; `pagedTerrainContentFeature` already does. `PagedTerrain` now exposes `residentPages`, `resident`, `residencyRevision`, `fallbackRevision`, `surfaceFallbackRevision` and `surfaceFallbacks` for uploaders.
