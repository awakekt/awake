- **A 2D particle emitter takes the `ParticleSpace` a 3D one takes (#1400).** `ParticleEmitter2D.space` and a scene file's `particle_emitter_2d` `space` take `ParticleSpace.World`, the default, or `ParticleSpace.Local`, the names `particle_emitter` uses. `ParticleEmitter2D.localSpace` and the `localSpace` field are deprecated: the property reads and writes `space`, and a scene file's `localSpace: true` still plays in local space and is written back as `space`. Binary compatibility is kept with hidden overloads of `SceneParticleEmitter2D`'s constructor and `copy`.
