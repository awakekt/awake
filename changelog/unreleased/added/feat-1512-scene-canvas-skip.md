- `SceneCanvas` can be drawn by a host that skips unchanged frames (`ComposeHost.skipUnchangedComposition` and `skipUnchangedMeasure`): it composes again when an element's content changes, through the element or straight to a component, when an element or canvas component is added, replaced, removed or swapped, when a node is reparented, and when the locale, images or fonts change, and every frame while an element follows a node, reads a binding, shows a camera's picture or is being held, dragged, moved or typed into (#1512). A still check costs 4 µs at 300 elements and 31 µs at 3,000 on desktop, and allocates nothing.
