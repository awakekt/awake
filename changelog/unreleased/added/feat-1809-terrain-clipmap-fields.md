- **A scene sets its terrain's geometry clipmap.** `SceneTerrain` gains `clipmapRings` (2 to 8), `clipmapRingResolution` (an even 16 to 256 cells a side) and `clipmapBaseSpacing` (metres, above 0), which reach `TerrainComponent.clipmapConfig`. They default to the clipmap every terrain drew with, 6 rings of 64 cells starting at 2 m, so a terrain without them draws as before; a terrain saved now writes them. A finer grid halves the vertex spacing at every distance: 1 km from the camera it is 32 m at 64 cells, 16 m at 128 and 8 m at 256. The terrain guide lists the vertex and triangle counts per setting.
