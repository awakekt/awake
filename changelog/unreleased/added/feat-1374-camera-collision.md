- **A third-person camera can keep out of the ground and walls (#1374).** A `camera_rig` that sets `collision` (`CameraRig.collision` in code) keeps its eye `groundMargin` above the terrain, sampled around the eye the size of the near plane, and sweeps a sphere of `radius` from the pivot to stop the eye just short of the first static body; it pulls in at once and eases back out over `easeOutSeconds`. A played project answers the ground from its `terrain` and `paged_terrain` heights and the obstacles from its physics world. The maths is `CameraCollisionSolver` in the new `awake:core:camera` module, usable without a scene. Off by default: a rig without `collision` moves exactly as before.
