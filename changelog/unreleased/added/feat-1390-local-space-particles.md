- **A 3D particle emitter can keep its particles with it (#1390).** `ParticleMotion.space` and a scene file's `particle_emitter` `space` take `ParticleSpace.World`, the default and the behaviour so far, or `ParticleSpace.Local`, which keeps particles relative to the emitter's entity and draws them through its current frame each frame, so they move, turn and scale with it: an aura on a moving bone instead of a trail. Gravity still pulls in world space and camera-facing sprites still face the camera; a local emitter takes no `ground`. `forEachLiveParticle` and `onParticleDeath` still report world positions.
