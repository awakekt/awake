- **Tinted and fading copies of a textured mesh draw as one instanced draw.** Copies that differ only in their `PbrMaterial` base colour or `DitherFade` opacity fold on the new `PipelineVariant.InstancedTinted`, each carrying its tint and fade in one `vec4` after its model matrix, and draw exactly as they do one by one; a played project's textured instanced pipeline uses it. Seven tints over 190 meshes drew 1,330 scene-pass draws and now draw 190. A custom instanced shader built for `PipelineVariant.Instanced` is unchanged and keeps such copies apart; to fold them, build its pipeline with `PipelineVariant.InstancedTinted` and read the `vec4` at the location after the matrix (`docs/reference/scene-instancing.md`). A backend says which pipelines tint through `GpuDrawPreparer.canTintInstances` (#1555).
