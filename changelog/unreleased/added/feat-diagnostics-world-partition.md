- **A world-partition debug source, and the debug tools guide.** `WorldPartitionSystem.probe` is a `WorldPartitionProbe` the system writes every update, with no allocation in steady frames: the observer and its cell, and each active cell's `WorldCellState` (`Reading` while its load is in flight, `Shown`, or `Leaving` once past the loading radius but inside the unloading one), with a count of each. `WorldPartitionDebugSource(probe)`, id `cells` (`--debug=cells`, `?debug=cells`), shows the observer's cell and the three counts on the debug panel and draws each active cell's outline coloured by its state, with the loading and unloading radius circles, around the observer; it reuses its points and colours, so drawing allocates nothing. Both are in `awake:world`, which now depends on `awake:core:diagnostics`. The new "Debug tools" guide covers probes and sources, writing one, turning sources on with `--debug=a,b` or `?debug=a,b`, `service(DebugRegistry::class, registry)`, the panel and the lines, and the zero cost of a source that is off (#1218).
