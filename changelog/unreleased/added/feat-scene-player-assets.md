- **Play an Awake project without the editor.** New `awake:scene:player`: `loadPlayableProject(files)` reads `awake.project.json`, validates it, reads the entry scene and loads its glTF models, and `SceneAppDsl.playProject(project)` plays it the way Awake Studio's Play does. WASD or the arrow keys move the `movement_control` entity relative to the camera, Space jumps, spinning entities turn, and the scene's camera follows the player on a third-person rig (`preparePlayCamera(world)` is public for hosts with their own loop). `builtInSceneAssets()` registers the meshes Studio's scenes name (`cube`, `sphere`, `ground`, `plane`, `checkered-floor`) and the `lit-shadow` material.
- **glTF models in scenes.** New `awake:scene:gltf`: `GltfAssetResolver` resolves `.gltf` and `.glb` paths in scenes to meshes and materials, including external `.bin` and image files, multi-material models and skinned models. It moves from Awake Studio, so a game loads the same models the author placed.
