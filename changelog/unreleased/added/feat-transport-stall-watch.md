- **Stalled connections are detected (`:awake:net:api`).** `TransportStallDetector` reports an upstream stall when a socket's send buffer (`DirectTransport.bufferedBytes`) holds bytes that haven't fallen for over 1 s, and a downstream stall when nothing has arrived for over 2 s while the client expects a stream. A gap between readings, such as a frozen thread or a background tab, restarts the wait instead of counting as a stall. `DirectTransport.watchStalls()` runs it on the socket's own timer and exposes a `StateFlow<TransportStallState>` that a reconnect policy or diagnostics panel can observe, plus an `onEnded` callback with each stall's length.
