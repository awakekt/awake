- **Prewarm a scene tree's assets before it spawns in `awake:scene:runtime`.** `SceneNode`, `ScenePrefab` and `SceneDocument` gain `assetReferences()`, which lists the meshes and materials, sprite, tilemap and 2D particle textures, and audio clips a tree names without putting it into a world. `SceneAppLifecycleRuntime.prewarm(node)` (or `SceneAssetLibrary.prewarm(runtime, references)`) builds and uploads its meshes and materials a frame at a time, up to `SceneAssetLibrary.prewarmFrameBudgetBytes` of new mesh a frame, and has the scene's resolvers load its textures off the frame. It returns a `ScenePrewarm` with progress, `failed`, a suspending `await()`, and `close()`, so a later `spawn` of the tree draws on its first frame and shares what was built. A prewarm holds what it built, so the retained-mesh budget can't evict it before the spawn, until it is closed; closing early, cancelling the coroutine that awaits it, or tearing down the scene stops it, and a ready prewarm never closed is logged as a warning at teardown. `SceneSession.coroutineScope` is new and public: a scope for work that lives as long as the scene's session, a child of the app's `AppCoroutineScope` when one is registered, cancelled when the session is disposed, and run on `SceneAppSpec.sessionDispatcher` (#1316).
