- **Every part of an on-screen control is a themable slot.** A stick's `base`, `knob`, `sprintRing` and `arrows`, an action button's `background`, `icon`, `label`, `cooldown`, `charges`, `aimCursor`, `aimRange` and `cancelZone`, and a look area's `feedback` are each a `CanvasSlot`: a style, images (single, nine-slice, atlas, per state, or `frames` played as a sprite animation), or a custom composable registered with `canvasSlotContent` and given the control's live state. Each `CanvasControlState` (`Idle`, `Pressed`, `Held`, `Disabled`, `CoolingDown`, `Ready`, `Aiming`, `Cancelling`) can change a slot's style, scale, opacity or frames, faded in over its `transitionSeconds`. `canvas_controls.theme` (`CanvasControlTheme`) restyles every control at once from a built-in preset, `MinimalFlat`, `Glass` or `Textured`, `@name` colour and image tokens, and a skin, and an element's own `skin` styles just that control. `canvas_controls.playerLayout` (`CanvasPlayerLayout`) scales, moves and fades controls by their new `controlId`, saved with `toJson()`. `previewState` shows one state for an editor's preview (#878).
