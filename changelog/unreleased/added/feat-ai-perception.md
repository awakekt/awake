- **AI agents perceive: sight cones, line of sight, hearing, memory and factions.** In `awake:ai`, a `Perceiver` sees within a focus cone and a wider peripheral cone (`SightSettings`), through a pluggable `LineOfSight`, hears `SoundEvent`s within a range scaled by loudness and muffled when blocked (`HearingSettings`), and keeps a `PerceptionMemory` of each entity's last known position and time that forgets after `forgetAfter` seconds and snapshots to data. A `FactionTable` of hostile, neutral and friendly relations decides how it regards what it perceives. `PerceptionSystem` runs it on a budget: each perceiver looks every `updateInterval` seconds, staggered, and at most `maxSightUpdates` look per update, fairly in turn; 1,000 agents among 1,000 targets cost about 0.6 ms an update on a desktop JVM. It writes the target, its position, whether a hostile is in sight, how many, and the latest noise to the agent's blackboard under configurable `PerceptionKeys`, and the registry gains the `ai.perception.sees`, `ai.perception.remembers` and `ai.perception.heard` conditions. All settings are `@Serializable`. `NavFieldLineOfSight` in `awake:ai:behavior` takes line of sight from a navigation grid, through the new `NavField.hasLineOfSight(from, to)` in `awake:navigation` (#877).
