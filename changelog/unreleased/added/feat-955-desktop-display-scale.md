- **Desktop UI follows the OS display scale (#955).** On desktop the UI density is now the window's content scale: 1.25 and 1.5 at Windows' 125% and 150%, `Xft.dpi` over 96 on X11, 2 on a Retina panel, as a browser's `devicePixelRatio` is on the web. It follows a window moving to a monitor with another scale, and text stays sharp at the fractional densities. A host that wants one framebuffer pixel per dp passes `desktopWindowOptions { displayScale = DesktopDisplayScale.Unscaled }` as `runVulkanDesktopGame`'s new `windowOptions`; `runDesktopWindow`'s options overload reports the density through its new `onDensity`.
