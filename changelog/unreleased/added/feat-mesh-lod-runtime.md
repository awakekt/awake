- **Levels of detail at run time, decided per entity.** A glTF model cooked with `MSFT_lod` (or with `_LOD1`-named levels) now draws them: its `mesh_renderer` gets an `LodGroup` that switches it by screen coverage, keeping its material, so copies at one level still fold into instanced draws. A skinned character's parts get levels too and switch together. Each level shares its full mesh's GPU vertex buffer through the new `GpuDevice.createMeshSharingVertices`. `LodGroup` gains a `LodPolicy` (`forcedLevel`, `minLevel`/`maxLevel`, `bias`, `shadowLevelOffset`), `follow` so one model switches with another, `minScreenCoverage`, and shadows can cast with a coarser level. `SceneLod.quality` is the host's global LOD bias. Each is read every frame. The selection logic is the new scene-free `awake:core:lod` module. In the render parity tests, eight copies of a model at increasing distances hand Vulkan and WebGPU 15,827 of 36,864 triangles and look the same as at full detail (#1185).
