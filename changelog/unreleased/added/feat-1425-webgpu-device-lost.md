- **WebGPU tells the host when its GPU device is lost (#1425).** The browser host watches `GPUDevice.lost` and, once, stops drawing, hands the loss to the app's `AppErrorHandler` as the new `AppErrorSource.GraphicsDevice` with a `WebGpuDeviceLostException` cause, and calls `WebGpuEngine.onDeviceLost` with the `WebGpuDeviceLost` reason and message. `GraphicsEngine.deviceLoss` and the protected `reportDeviceLost` hold the loss for every backend: a lost engine plays no frame, so nothing more is submitted. The device is not recreated; reload the page, or dispose the engine and launch a new one. A device the engine destroys on dispose is not reported. An exhaustive `when` over `AppErrorSource` needs a `GraphicsDevice` branch.
