- **A canvas scaler can snap its scale, so pixel art on a fractional scale is a clean multiple of itself.** `CanvasScaler.snap` (`None`, the default, `WholeStep` or `HalfStep`, also the `snap` field of `canvas_scaler`) rounds the scale the rule gives to the nearest whole number or half before `minScale`, `maxScale` and `playerScale` apply, and the canvas is laid out at the rounded scale: 1.4125 draws at 1.5 with `HalfStep` (6% larger) and at 1 with `WholeStep` (29% smaller). Text needs no snapping: a canvas already rounds each text element's size to whole device pixels after the scale and the density, so text up to 24 pixels draws from exact-size glyphs at any scale, now pinned by tests at 1.25, 1.4125, 1.5 and 1.775. Scenes saved without `snap` load and draw as before (#1028).
