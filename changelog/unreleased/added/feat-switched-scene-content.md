- **An editor's Play loads Core's content for a scene it switches to, every kind.** An editor holds Core's content for the scene it edits. When Play switched to another scene through a `SceneSwitch`, it had to load that scene's content one kind at a time, and a kind it missed didn't draw: a level's shader effects stayed blank after a menu loaded it. `loadCoreSceneContent(scene, files)` now reads everything Core's own capabilities load for a scene and nothing of a game's. `SceneContent.plus` and `ShaderEffectAssets.plus` lay the editor's own content, such as the documents being edited, over it. `ShaderEffectSystem` also tells apart a document that failed to load, now listed in `ShaderEffectAssets.failed`, from one that hasn't been loaded yet: it reports the second once, as not drawn yet, rather than as an error, and draws the effect once the document arrives.
