- **Keep chosen entities at full detail.** Tag an entity `DetailPin`, or give its scene node a `detail_pin` component, and it draws its `LodGroup`'s level 0 whatever its distance, and so does every group that follows it. `AnimationSystem` poses its skinned model every frame, whatever the `AnimationUpdateRate` would hold it for. An entity whose enabled `SocketAttachmentComponent` follows a pinned one (a weapon, a rider) is pinned too, up to four attachments deep. A game can then tighten the detail and animation bands for a crowd without the player's own character, its target or its mount dropping detail with it. Selection stays allocation-free with pins present. (#1805)
