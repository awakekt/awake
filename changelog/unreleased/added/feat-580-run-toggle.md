- **A player can toggle running with a key.** `movement_control` gains `runMode` and `runKey`. `Hold`, the default and today's behaviour, runs while `runKey` (default `Shift`) is held. `Toggle` starts running, and each press of `runKey` switches between walking and running, as in games where characters run unless the player chooses to walk. Typing into a text field leaves a toggle as it was. `MovementControl` carries both, and `PlayerInputSystem` reads them per player. On a touch screen, a `run` canvas_element Button does the same: held for `Hold`, pressed to switch for `Toggle`. A player's `runKey` can't be a key it moves or jumps with.
