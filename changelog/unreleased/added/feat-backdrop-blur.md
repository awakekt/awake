- **Frosted-glass backdrop blur, on Vulkan.** `UiDrawPrimitive.BackdropBlur` blurs what is already drawn behind a (rounded) rectangle or path, the 3D scene and the UI under it, then saturates, tints and grains it inside the shape. The blur is a dual-filter (Kawase) pass chain at reduced resolution whose radius is a Gaussian standard deviation, as CSS `blur()` (`DualKawaseBlur`, with `BackdropBlurReference` as its CPU twin). `Renderer.backdropBlurQuality` (`BackdropBlurQuality.Off`, `Low`, `Medium`, `High`) caps the halvings, the output level and the blurs per frame; it starts at `recommendedBackdropBlurQuality` for the device's `GpuDeviceClass`, so a software rasterizer fills every panel with its fallback tint. Panels that cannot see each other share one blur, a panel over another blurs again, and panels past the frame's limit fall back. WebGPU draws the fallback tint until its pass lands (#879).
