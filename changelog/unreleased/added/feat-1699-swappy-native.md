- The Android Vulkan JNI library links Swappy, the frame pacer from `games-frame-pacing` 2.1.3, and `com.awakekt.awake.vulkan.swappy.SwappyVk` exposes its Vulkan entry points (#1699). Nothing calls them yet. The setting that switches Android frame pacing between sleeping and Swappy follows, with sleeping the default. Swappy is linked from its static library with the bindings' own `c++_static`, and its symbols are hidden. The published Android artifact gains no dependency and needs no `libc++_shared.so`. `scripts/swappy-symbols.sh` checks both ABIs in CI.
