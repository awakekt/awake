- **Scene component bindings are generated at compile time.** Annotate a scene schema with `@SceneBinding(LiveComponent::class)` in a module that applies the `com.awakekt.awake.plugin.scene-components` build plugin, and a KSP processor writes its `SceneComponentBinding` (fields copied both ways by name, with `toComponent()` and `toSceneComponent()`) and the module's `SceneComponentModule` registry, such as `SceneCoreSceneComponents`, on every target. A field with no counterpart, two types with no `SceneValueConverter` between them, a scene component with no binding, or a public hand-written binding not marked `@RegisteredBinding` fails the build. `@RuntimeOnly`, `@SavedAs` and `@SavedOnly` mark fields, and `SceneBindingHooks` (`afterCopyToLive`, `afterCopyToSaved`, `afterAttach`, `beforeExport`) cover bindings that are mostly copies; asset, link and native bindings stay hand-written. `SceneComponentRegistry.register(module)` registers a whole module. The tag, spin control, static transform, fog, ambient light, tone mapping and light bindings are now generated and save the same bytes as before (#1089).
