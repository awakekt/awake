- **A bound text shows a whole number exactly, and can group its digits.** A `{component.field}` in a `canvas_element` binding's `text` used to read the field as a `Float`, so a whole number above 16,777,216, such as an amount of currency or experience, was shown rounded. A whole number now shows every digit, whatever its size. A format after a colon sets how a number is written: `{purse.gold:n0}` shows `1,234,567`, `{health.current:f0}` rounds to a whole number, and `{health.share:p0}` shows a fraction as a percentage. A format is a letter, `n` for grouped digits, `f` for the digits alone or `p` for a percentage, and a digit for the places after the point, so `n2` shows `1,234,567.50`. Without a format, text looks as it did. A format that is not one leaves its placeholder as written, scene validation reports it, and a field that is not a number ignores the format.
  - `NumberFormat` in `awake:core:text` is the formatter on its own: `NumberFormat.parse("n0")?.format(1234567L)`. It works from a number's decimal digits, so a `Long` is exact to its last digit and a `Double` rounds the way it reads (1.005 to two places is 1.01), half away from zero.
