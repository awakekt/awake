- `awake:scene:scene3d`, `awake:asset:shader-pack`, `awake:engine:render`: the sun and moon draw over any sky. `skybox.sunAndMoon`, `sunTexture`, `moonTexture` and `moonPhase` draw them through a new celestial pass (`celestialContentFeature`, `SkyboxCelestialSystem`, `PipelineVariant.BackgroundBlended`) over cubemap and flat-colour skies; a procedural sky with none of them keeps its own discs, unchanged. With a `day_cycle` the bodies follow its sun path (`DayCycle.sunDirection`), not the light's direction, so a rotated light node can hold the ground light still; a stop's `sunVisibility` and `moonVisibility` give each body its hours, and `additiveBodies` adds them to the sky. Content features read the pass's sun direction with `RenderFrameContext.writeSunDirection`, since `RenderFrameContext.light` is only a default on both backends (#1537).
