- **A host without a frame loop plays any of a project's scenes.** `LoadedProject.switchTo(path)` moves a loaded project to another of its scenes the way a `SceneSwitch` does inside `runProject`: the scene loads with the project's registry, capabilities, content and physics world factory, then the scene it leaves is closed and its physics world destroyed. A test, a server or a project check can now open each scene with its capabilities, with no rewritten manifest. A path that leaves the project, names no file or doesn't load throws `IllegalArgumentException` and leaves the playing scene as it was; a closed project, or one `runProject` plays, refuses with `IllegalStateException` (#925).
