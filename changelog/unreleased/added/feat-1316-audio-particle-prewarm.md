- **Prewarm a scene tree's audio clips and 3D particle images before it spawns.** A `ScenePrewarm` now decodes the `audio_source` clips a tree names, off the frame in `SceneSession.coroutineScope` and without playing them, and reads the images its `particle_emitter`s show, which `assetReferences()` lists apart in `particleTextures`. Both go into loads the host shares with the scene's systems through `SceneAssetLibrary.audioClips` and `SceneAssetLibrary.particleImages`: `audioClipLoads(files)` for `AudioSystem(player, loads)`, and `particleImageLoads(files)` for `ParticleContentSystem(renderer, loads)`. Each clip and image decodes once, whoever asks first, and the prewarm holds it until it is closed, so the first play and the first emitter draw after the spawn need no decode. Closing the prewarm or tearing the scene down cancels a decode nothing else waits for, and a clip or image that fails is listed in `failed`. The shared, cancellable load by path is `AssetLoads` in `awake:core:io`, and `loadAudioClip` and `loadParticleImage` load one file (#1316).
