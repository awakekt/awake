- `TextureUploads.observer`: when set, told about each image texture `createImageMaterial` uploads, with the name its caller gave it (scene sprites, tilemaps, trails, particles and glTF materials pass their texture's path) and how it went up: block-compressed in which format, copied on the GPU, or as RGBA8 from the CPU. For finding which texture draws wrong on a device. Null by default, which reports nothing (#1621).
