- **Physics: a raycast can skip one body, and the character's rays can look past the static layer.** `PhysicsWorld.raycast` has an overload taking `ignore: BodyHandle?`, skipped inside the query on every Jolt backend as `shapeCast` already does, so a ray cast from inside a body of the caller's own reports the nearest other body. `KinematicCharacterController.staticLayer` (also on the scene wrapper and `CharacterController`) names the layer its rays look for static ground on: the push-out guard, `liftOutOf` and `groundHeightBelow`. It defaults to `CollisionLayers.World`, as before; a game with static ground on its own layer sets that layer, and `null` looks at every layer while seeing through the character's own body (#1241).
