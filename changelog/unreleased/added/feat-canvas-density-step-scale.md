- **A `DensityStep` canvas scale mode.** A `CanvasScaler` (and a scene's `canvas_scaler`) in `CanvasScaleMode.DensityStep` draws a unit as the largest whole number of `pixelsPerUnit`-pixel steps that still leaves the box's short side at least `referenceHeight` units, and never less than one step: 1080 pixels against `720` is one pixel per unit, 2160 three, and a phone held either way gets the same scale. A desktop HUD keeps `720`; a touch one usually sets `360`. `snap` does not apply, since the scale is already a step; `minScale`, `maxScale` and `playerScale` do.
