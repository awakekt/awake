- **Compose backdrop blur source/effect pair with themable glass styles and presets.** Added ecosystem-standard source/effect backdrop blur to `:awake:compose:ui` (#879, #957). `Modifier.backdropSource(state)` and `rememberBackdropSourceState()` register content surfaces to be captured and blurred, while `Modifier.backdropEffect(source, style, shape)` applies a dual-filter frosted glass blur over the source content with full support for arbitrary shapes, rounded corners, and paths. Visual appearance is configured via `BackdropGlassStyle` (blur radius, tint, saturation, noise, fallback) with material presets (`UltraThin`, `Thin`, `Regular`, `Thick`, `UltraThick`), and `DrawScope.drawBackdropBlur(...)` in `:awake:compose:ui:graphics:drawscope`.
