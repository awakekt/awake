- **Free motion mode for the character controller.** `CharacterController.motionMode` switches a character between `CharacterMotionMode.Grounded` (walking, as before and the default) and `CharacterMotionMode.Free`, the primitive swimming, flying, no-clip-style spectating and similar movement build on: the intent's `moveX`/`moveY`/`moveZ` become a world velocity in units per second, gravity, ground snapping, step-up/down and the slope limit are off, the body is never turned, and the same capsule still collides with walls, floors, ceilings and terrain. Switching modes drops the fall speed; returning to walking finds the ground again. `CharacterController.lastContact` (a `CharacterContact` from `KinematicCharacterController.lastContact`) reports the last step's first contact normal as the sweep met it, before the slide, and whether anything lies below, so a game can bounce, damp or find the floor without becoming grounded. `freeMinY`/`freeMaxY` optionally bound free motion's height. The physics capability gains `KinematicCharacterController.moveFree`. Momentum, drag and any flight model stay with the game.
