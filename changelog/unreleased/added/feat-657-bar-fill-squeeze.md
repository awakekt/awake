- **A Bar's fill image can squeeze to its value instead of being cut at it.** A `canvas_element` Bar's `style.fillImage` is laid out at the bar's full width and cut at the value, so its right end cap shows only at 100%. `style.fillImageMode` now chooses: `Cut`, the default, keeps that, and `Squeeze` draws the fill's nine-slice at the value times the bar's width, so both end caps stay and only the middle stretches, as many games' gauges do. Below the two caps' combined width they shrink together rather than overlap, an empty bar draws no fill, and every piece lands on a whole pixel at any scale. A bar with no `fillImage` ignores the mode, and a scene that leaves it out looks as it did.
