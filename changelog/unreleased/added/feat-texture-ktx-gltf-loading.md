- **Cooked textures on a played project's models.** A glTF model's image that ships cooked to KTX2 beside it loads from the Basis Universal file on desktop and web, transcoded to the device's block format when its material is made, or decoded to RGBA8 where the device takes none. `GltfAssetResolver.setImageDecoder` takes a decoder tried before the image, `CookedTextureLoader` adds `withCookedImages()` and `decode(bytes)`, and `GpuImageSource.compressed(formats)` lets a source pick its block format at upload (#1211).
