- **Independent instances of one loaded project's scene (#1435).** `LoadedProject.newSceneInstance()` makes a `SceneInstance` with its own `World` (the scene's entities already in it), its own planned systems (`instance.sceneSystems(...)`, shaped like `LoadedProject.sceneSystems`) and, when the scene has bodies or characters, its own physics world from the project's `physicsWorld` factory. It shares, read-only, what the project loaded once: the scene document, component registry, capabilities, glTF models and scene content such as collision meshes and AI definitions. A server running many short-lived copies of one map loads the project once instead of once per copy. Making an instance reads no file and is headless-safe. `close()` ends it (systems, entities, then its physics world) and leaves the project, its other instances and the shared data untouched, and an instance keeps playing across the project's `switchTo` and `close()`.
