- **Cook-time mesh LOD.** The new `awake:asset:mesh-lod` module simplifies meshes with an attribute-aware quadric simplifier that keeps UVs, normals, tangents, vertex colours, skin joints and weights, and doesn't tear seams or borders, and writes 50%, 25% and 10% levels (configurable) into a glTF asset as `MSFT_lod` with screen-coverage hints. Assets that already carry `MSFT_lod` or `_LOD1`-named levels are kept as authored. A project's `awake.lod.json` sets the defaults and per-asset overrides or opt-outs, and `./gradlew :awake:asset:mesh-optimizer:cookLods` cooks a project's models. `GltfParser.parseMeshPrimitives` reads every primitive of a GLB or `.gltf` (#1185).
