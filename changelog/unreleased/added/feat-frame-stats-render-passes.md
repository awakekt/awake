- **Per-pass draw counts show in the frame stats.** `SceneFrameStats.passes` and `SceneFrameStats.renderPasses` carry the renderer's `framePassStats` and `frameRenderPasses` (null when headless or uncounted), `FrameBreakdownSample.passes` (a new `FramePassSample`) and `renderPasses` feed the `sceneOpaqueDraws`, `sceneTransparentDraws`, `shadowDraws`, `shadowPasses`, `uiDraws`, `otherDraws` and `renderPasses` keys of `FrameBreakdown`, and the engine showcase's stats card shows them, so a profile tells where a frame's draws went: scene, shadow cascades, or UI (#1545).
