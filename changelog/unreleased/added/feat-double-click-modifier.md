- **Double click in `awake:compose:foundation`: `Modifier.doubleClickable` and `DoubleClickTracker`.** `doubleClickable(onClick) { onDoubleClick() }` fires on two clicks within 0.4 s and 8 px of each other, timed on the composition's frame clock, and runs a single click at once instead of holding it back. `DoubleClickTracker` is the same rule as plain state, for a canvas, list or 3D hit test that already turns presses into clicks. Until now only `BasicTextField` knew a double click.
