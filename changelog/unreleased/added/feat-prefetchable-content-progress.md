- **Loading progress through wrapped content sources.** `PrefetchableAssetSource` is the contract a content source implements to fetch files ahead of their reads and count the bytes: `IndexedAssetSource` implements it, and `LockVerifiedAssetSource` passes it on to the source it wraps, so a project loaded through `verifiedByAssetsLock()` reports scene-switch and `loadProject` progress in steps instead of jumping from 0 to 1, with every asset still checked against the lock as it's read. A wrapper over a source that can't prefetch still reports 0 and then 1 (#1137).
