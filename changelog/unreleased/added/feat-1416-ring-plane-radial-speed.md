- **A tilted spawn ring's `radialSpeed` can push outward in the ring's own plane (#1416).** `ParticleMotion.radialInRingPlane` and a scene file's `particle_emitter` `radialInRingPlane` push `radialSpeed` away from the origin across the spawn ring that `orientSpawnRing` or `inheritOrientation` turns, instead of horizontally: an outward ripple across a sloped surface. Off by default, so `radialSpeed` still pushes horizontally.
