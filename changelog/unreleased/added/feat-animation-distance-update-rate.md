- **Distance-based animation update rate.** `AnimationSystem` now takes an optional `AnimationUpdateRate` so a consuming app can pose far skinned models less often: within a near distance they pose every frame as before, beyond a far distance their joint palette is held for an authored interval, easing linearly between. The clip's clock keeps advancing, so playback timing is unchanged, and `AnimationUpdateRate.EveryFrame` (the default) preserves current behavior. The renderer records each drawn model's camera distance for it, so the throttle needs no camera of its own. Cuts the per-frame `jointPalette` cost for a distant crowd of skinned characters (#1223).
