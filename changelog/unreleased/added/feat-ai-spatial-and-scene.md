- **Data-driven AI walks, and scenes name their trees and machines.** `registerSpatialKinds(placement)` in `awake:ai:behavior` adds `ai.move-to`, `ai.follow`, `ai.flee`, `ai.wander`, `ai.face`, `ai.distance` and `ai.in-area` to an `AiRegistry`. They target a blackboard key holding an entity or a position, such as `perception.target`, or a fixed point, and the moving ones route through the agent's `PathRequest` as patrol and chase do. `AgentPlacement` gains `face`, which `TransformAgentPlacement` and `MovementAgentPlacement` answer by setting the yaw. The new `behavior_tree` and `state_machine` scene components (`SceneBehaviorTree`, `SceneStateMachine`) name a definition file. A played project reads and checks every such file when it loads, refusing one with a problem and naming the file and node, then runs the trees and machines with Core's and the spatial kinds. Outside a project, `AiDefinitionLibrary` in `awake:ai` holds compiled definitions by path and `AiDefinitionAttachSystem` in `awake:scene:ai` builds entities from it, rebuilding them when a file is loaded again (#875).
