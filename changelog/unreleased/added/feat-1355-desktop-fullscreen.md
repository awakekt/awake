- **Desktop fullscreen (#1355).** `desktopHostActions(window)` takes the GLFW window handle `runDesktopWindow` passes to `onCreate`, and fills `HostActions.fullscreen` with `glfwFullscreen(window)`: borderless fullscreen on the monitor under the window's centre at its current video mode, so entering never switches the display mode. Leaving restores the windowed position and size from the last `enter`, and the swapchain follows through the framebuffer size as it does for any resize. `desktopHostActions()` with no window is unchanged, with `fullscreen` still null. `GlfwWindow` gains `glfwIsWindowFullscreen`, `glfwGetWindowBounds`, `glfwSetWindowFullscreen` and `glfwSetWindowWindowed`, in the new `GlfwFullscreen_jni.cpp` of `libawake-window`. Rebuild it with `./gradlew :awake:engine:window:buildDesktopNative`.
