- Text in the 3D world (#1062). Name plates (canvas followers scaled by distance) are laid out again at their final on-screen size, rounded to 16 sizes per doubling with hysteresis, land on whole device pixels while they move slowly, and fade without a layer that cuts their ink; planning a hundred of them allocates nothing per frame. New `awake:worldtext` capability and `world_text` scene component (`awake:scene:worldtext`): text laid out once and drawn as distance-field quads in the scene pass, edge width from screen-space derivatives, optional billboard mode, depth tested and sorted with other transparent draws, on Vulkan and WebGPU through `worldTextScenePipeline()`, which `ProjectRenderPlan` now includes. Adds `VertexFormat.PositionUvColor`.
