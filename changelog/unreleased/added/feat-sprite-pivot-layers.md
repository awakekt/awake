- **Sprites turn about a pivot, draw on named sorting layers, and play their clips in `sceneSystemsFor`.** A `sprite`'s new `pivot` (a fraction of its frame from the bottom left, the centre by default) is the point placed at the node, which it turns and scales about, for every frame of its sheet; a flipped sprite mirrors about it. The project manifest's new `sortingLayers` lists named layers back to front, and a `sprite` or `tilemap` names one in `sortingLayer` (`"Default"` by default): a later layer draws over an earlier one, and `sortOrder` orders within a layer, then depth from the camera. A layer the list leaves out draws on `Default` and is a warning when the project loads; a project without the list draws as before. The render contract gains `sortLayer` on `GpuDrawRequest` and `PreparedDraw`, and `SceneAppLifecycleRuntime.sortingLayers` carries the project's layers to the sprite and tilemap draws. `sceneSystemsFor` now runs `SpriteClipSystem` for a scene with `sprite_clips`, so an editor's Play advances them; `runProject` leaves it out when the app's infrastructure already runs one, which `SceneAppLifecycleRuntime.hasInfrastructureSystem` reports (#934).
