- **A steady frame rate on Android phones.** Set `AndroidFrameLoop.steadyRate` to `SteadyRate.Auto` and, while the frame-rate mode is `FrameRateMode.Auto`, frames run at the fastest rate the panel holds evenly that their cost fits. On a 120 Hz panel that is 120, or 60 for frames costing 12 to 16 ms, instead of an unpaced mix of one-refresh and two-refresh frames that reads as 60 to 83 and keeps changing. With Swappy pacing, 40 and 30 are candidates too. The window is asked for the rate (`Surface.setFrameRate`, fixed source), so the panel can switch to it. The rate steps down once the p90 frame cost passes 95% of its budget, steps up once the last 2 s fit under 75% of the faster one's, and never changes twice within 2 s. A frame's cost is its own work: the renderer reports its fence, acquire and present waits and its GPU time through `FrameCostReport`. `SteadyRate.Fixed(fps)` holds one rate. `AndroidFrameLoop.steadyRateStatus` gives a HUD the rate, why it was chosen, the p90 cost, the candidates, the panel's refresh rate and what holds it. `SteadyRate.Off` stays the default, so nothing changes until an app opts in; desktop and web are untouched.
