- **Undecorated (frameless) desktop windows (#1357).** A new `runDesktopWindow(game, decoration, …)` overload opens the window with `WindowDecoration.System` (the OS title bar and border) or `WindowDecoration.None` (frameless). The existing `runDesktopWindow` is unchanged and stays decorated. `windowDecoration(window)` and `setWindowDecoration(window, decoration)` read and change it at runtime, and a fullscreen window leaves fullscreen with the decoration it had. With no title bar the OS can't move the window, so `glfwWindowDrag(window)` returns a `WindowDrag`: call `update(dragging)` each frame while the player holds the game's own drag area, and the window follows the pointer. It does nothing on a fullscreen window or on Wayland, and resizing from the edges isn't offered. `GlfwWindow` gains `glfwSetWindowDecorated` and `glfwSetWindowPos`, in the new `GlfwWindowFrame_jni.cpp`. Rebuild `libawake-window` with `./gradlew :awake:engine:window:buildDesktopNative`.
