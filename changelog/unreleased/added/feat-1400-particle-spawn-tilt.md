- **A world-space emitter's spawn ring can tilt with its entity (#1400).** `ParticleMotion.orientSpawnRing` and a scene file's `particle_emitter` `orientSpawnRing` turn the spawn ring alone by the entity's rotation, so it lies in the plane its `Flat` sprites lie in, while `velocity`, `acceleration` and `radialSpeed` stay in world space: a circle laid on a sloped surface whose sparks still rise straight up. `inheritOrientation` still turns the ring and the velocity together, and a `Local` emitter's ring already lies in its entity's axes. Off by default.
