- **Trails: a strip of point pairs whose vertices and colours change every frame (#1465).** New `awake:trail` capability, with no scene dependency: `TrailRenderer` holds a `TrailStrip` of up to `capacity` pairs, each two points with their own straight-alpha colours, a visible count, `TrailBlend.Additive` or `Alpha`, a tint and an optional texture (`u` along, `v` across). `TrailBatch` draws each trail unlit in the transparent pass through its world matrix, culled by the box around its visible pairs, from one GPU buffer sized once at `capacity`: a frame uploads only the pairs that changed, changing `visible` alone uploads nothing, and once warm nothing is allocated per frame, with 64 trails of 300 pairs. `setSmoothed` and `catmullRom` turn key pairs into a smooth ribbon. The render contract gains `DynamicMesh` and the optional `GpuDynamicMeshes` capability, implemented on Vulkan with one host-visible vertex buffer per frame in flight; `VertexFormat.PositionRgbaUv`, `trailScenePipeline()` (in `ProjectRenderPlan`) and `DrawUniformPlan.Trail` draw it.
