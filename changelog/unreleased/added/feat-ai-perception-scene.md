- **Perception in scenes, through physics, at scale.** The `perceiver`, `perceivable` and `factions` scene components (`ScenePerceiver`, `ScenePerceivable`, `SceneFactions`) put perception in scene documents, and a played project runs it for them: entities are placed and faced by their transforms (`TransformPerceptionSpace`), and sight is blocked by the physics world when there is one and by the navigation grid otherwise. `PhysicsLineOfSight` in `awake:ai:behavior` answers line of sight with physics rays, seeing past the viewer's own body and counting the target's as reaching it. `PerceptionSystem` buckets perceivable entities into cells once per update (`indexCellSize`), so a look reads only those within its reach, and takes a `sightBudget` that stops looks once that much time has passed in an update, beside `maxSightUpdates`. `LineOfSight.isClear` now also receives the `World`, so a physics answer can find each entity's body (#877).
