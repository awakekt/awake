- **Tinted and fading copies of a mesh cast as one shadow draw.** The shadow passes fold the scene's opaque casters again by what a depth pass reads (mesh, material, cull mode, vertex animation), so copies the scene pass keeps apart for their tint or dither fade cast as one instanced draw. Under a shadowing sun, 3,000 renderers on 190 meshes with seven tints drew 1,330 draws into every cascade and now draw 190. `docs/reference/scene-instancing.md` says what folds, the caps per backend and how to see why a draw did not fold (#1555).
