- **Texture cook step.** The new `awake:asset:texture-cook` tool (`cookTextures`) compresses a project's PNG and JPEG textures to KTX2 with libktx, through LWJGL: ETC1S for colour, UASTC with Zstandard for normal maps and data, with mips filtered in linear light (renormalised for normal maps), and ASTC 4x4 and ETC2 files for mobile. `awake:asset:texture-ktx` adds `ProjectTextureSettings`, the project's `awake.textures.json`: defaults, per-texture overrides and opt-outs (#1211).
