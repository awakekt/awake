- **A Joystick has modes, a dead zone, a response curve and a sprint threshold.** A canvas Joystick's new `joystick` settings (`CanvasJoystick`) make it `Fixed`, `Floating` (the pad appears where the thumb lands in the element's zone) or `Dynamic` (dragged along past its reach), with a `radius`, `deadZone`, a `Linear`, `Quadratic` or `Cubic` `response` or a `curve` of points, `Four` or `Eight` way `directions`, `snapBack`, and an idle fade. Past its `sprintThreshold` it reports `sprinting` and `consumeSprintStart()`, and a played project holds its `sprintAction`. Each stick now follows only the pointer that landed on it first, so a second thumb pressing a button never moves it, and a fixed stick deflects from its centre wherever the thumb lands rather than from the touch point. Older scenes read unchanged (#878).
