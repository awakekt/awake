- **A radial sweep, a game-style progress bar and a number stepper, in Compose and on the canvas.** `ui:shadcn` adds `ShadcnRadialSweep` (a pie or ring from 0 to 1, a start angle, either direction, cut to any `DrawShape`), `ShadcnProgressBar` (horizontal, vertical or radial, with a trailing fill, segments or ticks, a label, and nine-slice art whose caps stay whole) and `ShadcnNumberStepper` (minus and plus around an editable field, min, max and step, a hold that repeats, and keys). On the canvas, a `Bar`'s new `bar` settings do the same, and the new `Sweep` and `Stepper` kinds take `sweep` and `stepper` settings; each binds through `bind.value`. The action button's cooldown sweep is now the same drawing. They share `sweepPath` (`core:graphics2d`), `TrailingValue` and `animateTrailing` (`compose:foundation`), and `NumberStepping` and `HeldStep` (`core:input`), which also map a controller's pad to steps (#888).
