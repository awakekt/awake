- **Canvas followers hide behind walls.** A follower whose `followRules.occlusion` is `true` now hides in a played project while a physics body stands between the camera and the point it follows, its followed node's own body, its character's, and a body the camera is inside left out. `PhysicsCanvasOcclusion` casts the rays within a budget: a new `canvas_follow_occlusion` component sets the most rays a frame, how many frames an answer stands, and the one collision layer they hit. The longest-waiting answers are cast first. `LineOfSightRays` in `awake:physics:api` is the budgeted, cached ray query behind it, for any caller with many askers a frame. `CanvasProjector.occluded(target, x, y, z)` passes the followed node, and a host with another answer, such as a depth read-back, sets its own `CanvasOcclusion` with `world.setCanvasOcclusion`. `CharacterController.body` exposes a character's own body.
