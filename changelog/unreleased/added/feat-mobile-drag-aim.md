- **Action buttons drag to aim.** A canvas Button's `button.aim` (`CanvasAim`) makes a drag away from it aim, as a `Direction` or a `Ground` target out to its `radius`, past a `deadZone`, and letting go fires its `action` with that aim wherever the thumb is. While it is dragged, the element reports `aiming`, `aimX` and `aimY`, and the canvas draws the reach, a cursor and a `cancel` zone; letting go over the zone cancels the shot (`consumeAimCancel()`). A played project pushes the aim to its axis `aimAction`. With `lockTarget`, a code-set `aimLock` (`CanvasAimLock`) locks the aim onto a target the game picks, named by `aimTarget` (#878).
