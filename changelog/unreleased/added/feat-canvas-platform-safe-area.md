- **The scene canvas keeps clear of a notch, and a physical-size HUD is true to the screen, on every platform without host code.** Each window host now reports the safe area and the screen's real dots per inch to the engine. Android reports its system bars and display cutout plus its display metrics' dpi. iOS reports its view's safe area. The browser reports `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, and the templates' and engine showcase's pages now set `viewport-fit=cover`. A desktop window reports the dpi of the monitor it is on. Each `AppFrame` carries them as `safeArea` (`DisplayInsets`) and `dpi` (0 where unknown), and `WindowLifecycle.setSafeArea`/`setDpi` are how a host reports them. `SceneAppLifecycleRuntime` hands them to the canvas unless a host sets `canvasSafeArea` or the new `canvasDpi`; `canvasSafeArea` is now nullable, and null uses the platform's. `CanvasOverlay` and `SceneCanvas` take the dpi a `ConstantPhysicalSize` `canvas_scaler` uses.
