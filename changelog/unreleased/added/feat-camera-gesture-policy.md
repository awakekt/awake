- A scene picks which pointer button orbits its camera: `camera_rig.orbitButtons` (`CameraRig.orbitButtons`, `CameraOrbitButtons` in `awake:core:camera`) is `PrimaryOrSecondary`, the default and today's behaviour, or `SecondaryOnly`, which leaves a primary drag to the game, for a client whose primary button walks and steers. One finger on a touch screen still orbits under either. A played project reads it from the scene, so no host needs its own `CameraSystem`.
