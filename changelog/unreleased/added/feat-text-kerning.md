- **Text is pair-kerned, as a browser kerns it.** Fonts are read for pair kerning when they load: the x-advance adjustments of the `GPOS` `kern` feature's pair positioning lookups (formats 1 and 2), or a `kern` table's format 0 subtables for a font without them. Measuring, painting, line breaking, ellipsizing, the caret, hit testing and selection all take the same kerned pen, so "AVATAR", "To Ty Yo" and "WAVE Tower" at 14 px now measure 50.76, 52.77 and 79.08 px, within 0.02 px of Chrome (were 53.29, 54.65 and 80.92), and a caret between two kerned letters sits where the second one starts. Exact-size pixel glyphs move by the kerning before each is snapped to its whole pixel. The built-in Roboto family carries its kerning in the outline subset `tools/fonts-tooling/subset_roboto_outlines.py` embeds (459 to 462 pairs a weight, about 2.6 KB a face), and `UiFonts.load` reads a project font's own. Kerning is on by default; `TextStyle(kerning = false)` turns it off for a subtree, as `font-kerning: none` does. `UiFont` gains `kerningFor`, `kerningBefore` is the shared per-pair step, `measureTextWidth` and `ellipsize` gain overloads taking `kerning`, and `TextStyle` gains `kerning`, which changes its constructor and `copy` JVM signatures. The layout conformance suite's 205 kerning metrics now pass against Chrome, so its expected-failure signature for this issue is gone (#1022).
