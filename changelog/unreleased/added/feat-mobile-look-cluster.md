- **A canvas look area and a button cluster layout.** The new `LookArea` kind is a region where one thumb's swipe looks and two thumbs pinch to zoom, read through `consumeLook()` and `consumeZoom()` and shaped by its `look` settings (`CanvasLook`: `sensitivity`, `invertX`, `invertY`, `pinchZoom`, `zoomSensitivity`). In a played project its swipe turns the active camera rig and its pinch zooms it, and each is pushed to the axis actions it names. `CameraRig.turnBy` and `CameraRig.zoomBy` turn and zoom a rig from code. An element's new `cluster` (`CanvasCluster`) places its children as a button cluster: the first on a pivot and the rest on an arc around it (#878).
