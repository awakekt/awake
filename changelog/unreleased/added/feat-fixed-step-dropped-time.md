- **The fixed-step loop reports the time it drops, and its cap is configurable.** A frame runs at most 5 fixed steps and drops the rest of its time, as before. A scene can now raise that cap with `maxFixedStepsPerFrame(...)` in its scene block (or `SceneAppSpec.maxFixedStepsPerFrame`). The loop now reports each frame: `FixedTimestepLoop` implements the new `FixedStepReport`, which gives `stepsThisFrame`, the frame's `droppedSeconds` and whole `droppedSteps`, a running `droppedStepsTotal`, and a `TimeDroppedListener` that is called once for each frame that drops time. Systems read it from `SceneAppLifecycleRuntime.fixedStep`, and a capability's systems from `SceneHostServices.fixedStep`, which `runProject` fills. A host that steps the systems itself passes its own loop with `withFixedStep` or `LoadedProject.sceneSystems(..., fixedStep = loop)`. A networked game that predicts its local player can now send or replay the steps a long frame dropped, without copying Core's cap and working the dropped steps out itself. Nothing is allocated per frame, and the default behaviour is unchanged (#1292).
