- **Stall detection works on every transport (`:awake:net:api`).** `Transport.watchStalls(scope)` returns a `StallWatch` that fits the transport. A `DirectTransport` gets `TransportStallWatch`, which watches both directions on the socket's own thread. Any other transport, such as the Ktor WebSocket client on the JVM and Android, gets the new `PolledStallWatch`. It's a coroutine that watches downstream only, because such a socket can't say how many bytes it holds unsent. Both expose the same `state: StateFlow<TransportStallState>`, `expectInbound`, `received()` and `onEnded`.
