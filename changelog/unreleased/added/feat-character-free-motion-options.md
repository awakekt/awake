- **Free motion options for the character controller.** `CharacterController.freeHalfHeight` (and `KinematicCharacterController.freeHalfHeight`) gives free motion a shorter capsule of the same radius, so a flyer fits under arches and low overhangs; the feet stay put, entering free motion shrinks it, and walking again grows it back once a headroom sweep finds room, staying short under a low ceiling until it walks out. `freeCollidesWithMoving = false` makes free motion sweep static geometry only, passing through every moving body (other characters' bodies, crates, moving platforms) while walls, floors and terrain still stop it. `groundHeightBelow(maxDistance)` returns the height of the first static surface straight below the character, one ray against static geometry, for an altimeter or a landing check. All are run-time state, not saved with the scene, and `CharacterConfig` is unchanged (#1239).
