- **Canvas followers scale to a crowd.** An element's new `followRules` keep many world-following elements cheap and readable. A follower wholly off the screen, behind the camera by its view depth, beyond `maxDistance`, or occluded through a host's `CanvasProjector.occluded` hook is left out. `scale` and `fade` curves size and fade it by distance, and with `pinToEdge` a follower whose target is off screen or behind the camera holds to the screen's edge, reporting `pinned` and `pinAngle`. A child with `followArrow` points to the target from there. A new `canvas_follow_limit` component, or `CanvasOverlay(followLimits = …)`, draws at most `max` of a group, keeping the nearest, the highest `priority`, or the highest `followScore` game code sets. `CanvasFollowerPool` hands follower entities out and takes them back hidden, so damage numbers and name plates make no entities as they come and go. `CanvasResolver.project` and `CanvasProjector.projectFollow` report a `CanvasProjection` with depth, distance and whether the point is behind the camera. A scene saved before these draws its followers as before, except that one wholly off the screen is no longer composed.
