- Text outlines and shadows draw from the font's distance field in one draw a glyph (#1061): a thick outline stays round with no gaps between stamps, a `TextShadow` (and a `canvas_element` `textShadow`) can take a `blur` for a soft shadow or a glow, and outlined, shadowed text costs one quad a glyph on Vulkan, WebGPU and the CPU snapshot raster. The built-in Roboto faces and fonts from `UiFonts.load` carry a coarse effect field beside their atlas for it; other fonts stamp as before.
