- **Compressed textures on Vulkan.** The Vulkan backend answers `GpuCompressedTextures`: a BC1, BC3, BC7, ETC2 or ASTC 4x4 image is uploaded as it is, with its mips, and bound as a material's base colour, so `createKtx2Material` keeps the texture compressed in GPU memory. The formats are the families the device enables (`textureCompressionBC`, `ETC2`, `ASTC_LDR`); on desktop Vulkan, Android and MoltenVK alike. A block format is sampled as UNORM, as RGBA8 is, since Awake's shaders decode sRGB themselves (#1211).
