- **A played project's scenes prewarm their audio clips and particle images with no host code.** `runProject` gives each scene it plays its own shared clips and particle images, seeded with what the scene read when it loaded. The scene's `AudioSystem` and `ParticleContentSystem` use them, they are registered as the runtime's `SceneAssetLibrary.audioClips` and `particleImages`, and they are closed when the scene stops or switches. A prewarm in a played scene therefore decodes into what its systems play and draw. `CoreSceneContent.AudioClipLoads` and `ParticleImageLoads` let a host that builds the systems itself give its own (#1316).
