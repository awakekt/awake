- Let a routed app declare a route whose scene is supplied after it starts: `deferredRoute` and `DeferredSceneRoute` load the scene the first time the route is entered (or `preload`ed), off the frame thread, showing the route's loading scene and reporting `routeStatus` meanwhile, and `projectRoute` plays a project loaded then, such as protected content read once the player has signed in. A failed load stays on the loading scene until the route is entered again, a load the player leaves waits for the next entry, and routes declared with `route` load before the app starts as before.
