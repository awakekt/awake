- **3D free-motion locomotion clips in `LocomotionPlayer` and scene free-motion binding (`#1314`).** `LocomotionConfig.free` (`FreeLocomotionConfig`) and `LocomotionPlayer.update(..., freeMotion = true)` select `idle`, `move`, `fast`, and optional directional `ascend` and `descend` clips from exponentially smoothed 3D `speed` and vertical velocity, with optional `crossFade` override, `moveReferenceSpeed` and `fastReferenceSpeed` playback scaling, 3D teleport filtering, external-clip yielding, and clean `Grounded <-> Free` transitions without firing `takeOff` or `land` one-shots. In the scene layer, `GroundContact.freeMotion` carries free-motion state from `CharacterControllerSystem` (`CharacterMotionMode.Free`) to `LocomotionAnimationSystem`, authored on `locomotion_animation` via `SceneLocomotionAnimation.free` (`SceneFreeLocomotionAnimation`) (#1314).
