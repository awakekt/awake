- **Backend-neutral image-pass layer (`ImagePassChain`) and `:awake:engine:render:effects`.** `:awake:engine:render:contract` now declares a generic multi-step image-pass contract (`ImagePassChain`, `ImagePassStep`, `ImagePassTargetSpec`, `ImagePassSource`, `ImagePassDestination`, `ImagePassShaderProgram`, `ImagePassPlacement`, `ImagePassTargetContext`, `GpuDeviceClass`, and `EffectQuality`), executed once in Vulkan (`VulkanImagePassExecutor`) and once in WebGPU (`WebGpuImagePassExecutor`) with pooled transient targets and mid-pass snapshot/resume (`loadOp = LOAD`). Shared effects live in the new `:awake:engine:render:effects` module (`TintImageEffect`, `ImageEffectShaders`, `ImagePassShaderRegistry`), and `VerifyBackendLayeringTask` forbids effect-specific names and imports in `render:contract` and both backends (D40, #957).
