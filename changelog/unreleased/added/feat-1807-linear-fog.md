- **Linear distance fog.** `Fog` and `SceneFog` gain `mode` (`FogMode.Exponential`, the default, or `FogMode.Linear`) and `start` and `end` in metres from the eye. A linear fog leaves everything nearer than `start` clear and reaches the fog colour at `end`, in the `textured` and `lit_shadow` shaders on Vulkan and WebGPU. All three fields may be written at runtime and take effect on the next frame. A range the shaders cannot ramp across (a negative or non-finite start, or an end at or before it) is refused. A document without the fields fogs exponentially, exactly as before. The frame uniform block gains a last `fogRange` field (16 bytes), and `GpuEnvironmentState`, `EnvironmentUniforms` and `FrameUniformBlock.pack` gain `fogStart` and `fogEnd` (linear while `fogEnd` is beyond `fogStart`, both 0 by default), all additive.
