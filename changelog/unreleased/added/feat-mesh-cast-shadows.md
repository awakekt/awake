- **A mesh can opt out of casting shadows.** `MeshRenderer.castsShadows` (default `true`, so existing scenes are unchanged) keeps a mesh out of every shadow map, whether the camera sees it or not, while it still draws and still receives shadows: decals, ground cards, small props, distant filler. Scene documents carry it as `mesh_renderer.castsShadows`, and a document without the field still casts. `LodGroup` and `ModularCharacterComponent` take the same flag. Scene culling no longer draws such a mesh as a shadow-only caster when it is off screen, and `ScenePassCompiler` leaves draws whose new `GpuDrawRequest.castsShadows` is false out of the cascade and point-shadow passes and out of the cascade near-plane fit (#997).
