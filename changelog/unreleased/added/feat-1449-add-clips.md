- **Clips join a running animation player without a pop (#1449).** `AnimationPlayer.addClips(clips)` adds clips to a player while it plays, keeping the playhead, the crossfade in progress and the playback mode, so a clip loaded after the model started (a streamed skill clip, say) plays or fades in next from the pose on screen instead of a rebuilt player snapping for a frame. `AnimationPlayer.playback` reports whether the active clip loops or plays once, and `AnimationClip.retarget(from, to)` rebinds a clip authored on another skeleton by joint name, dropping channels whose joint the target lacks.
