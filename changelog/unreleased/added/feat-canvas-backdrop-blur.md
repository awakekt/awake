- Add frosted-glass canvas elements: `CanvasStyle.backdrop` (`CanvasBackdrop`: `blur`, `tint`, `saturation`, `fallback`, also `canvas_element.style.backdrop` in a scene document) blurs whatever is drawn beneath the element, the 3D scene included, in its corner-radius shape on Vulkan and WebGPU, sharing one blur between panels that do not overlap; where blur does not run, or the host provides `LocalCanvasBackdropBlur` false, it draws `fallback` with no extra pass.
