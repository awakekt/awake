- **A canvas tells a host the order it draws in and what is on top at a point.** An editor that picks a canvas element under a click had to copy `CanvasOverlay`'s rules: a child over its parent, a higher `order` over a lower one, and the later of two equals over the earlier. `canvasDrawOrder(items)` and `canvasItemAt(items, x, y)` now answer from those rules for any host's items, and `world.canvasDrawOrder(siblingOrder)` and `world.canvasElementAt(x, y, siblingOrder)` answer for a `World`, with the same sibling order `SceneCanvas` takes. `CanvasAnchor` and the canvas README now also state how a centred element lands on whole pixels: when the spare room is odd, the extra pixel goes on the right or at the bottom, so an editor can round the same way.
