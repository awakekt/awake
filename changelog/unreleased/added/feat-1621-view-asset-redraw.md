- A canvas View that keeps its picture (`CanvasViewRefresh.OnRequest`) now draws again by itself, once, when what it shows settles: a renderable attaching inside its view, such as a spawned model that loaded late, or a texture the scene waited for loading or being forgotten for a hot reload. Models attaching outside its view leave it alone. A host no longer needs to redraw on a timer to catch late assets, though a material changed on a renderer that is already attached still needs `invalidateCanvasViews` (#1621).
