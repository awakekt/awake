- **A scene's `trail_renderer` draws a trail at its node (#1465).** New `awake:scene:trail`: the `trail_renderer` component (`capacity`, `blend`, `tint`) attaches a `TrailRenderer`, and `TrailRenderFeature` draws every entity's trail through its transform, which a played scene runs with nothing wired by the host. A game's systems write its pairs, visible count and colours every frame. A host with no renderer builds no trail buffers.
