- `awake:compose:foundation` ships `SkinnedButton`, `SkinnedFrame` and `SkinnedBar`, which read `LocalSkin` and fall back to a token colour with no skin or no such part, so one layout serves an image skin and a colour theme. `SkinnedKeys` names the parts they read (check a skin with `skin.requireParts(SkinnedKeys.All)`); `Skin.sliceInsets` and `Skin.pixelsPerDp` let a layout pad by the art.
