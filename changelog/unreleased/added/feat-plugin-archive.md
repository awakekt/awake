- **Plugin packaging and trust formats in one public module, `com.awakekt.awake:plugin-archive`.** It holds `plugin.json` (`PluginManifest`), the `.awakeplugin` zip with a reader for every target (at most 4096 entries and 64 MiB unpacked, safe unique paths), archive signature checks, root-signed `PublisherKeyList` and `PluginRevocationList` documents (`{document, signature}`, SHA256withRSA, domain-separated), one `PluginTrust` decision (verified by publisher, unsigned, bad signature, revoked), and `plugins.lock.json` (`AwakePluginsLock`). Signatures and digests are checked by each platform's own provider through cryptography-kotlin 0.6.0, so verification now really runs on Android, iOS and the browser (WebCrypto); the checks are `suspend` on every target. Archives signed before the move still verify. Decision D41.
