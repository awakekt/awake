- **Touch controls show by the device last used, and every control is a thumb's size.** A scene's new `canvas_controls` component (`CanvasControls`) sets `touchVisibility`: `Auto` shows `touchOnly` elements once a finger touches the screen and hides them once a controller, a key or the mouse is used, switching live, while `Host`, `Always` and `Never` keep or override the host's choice. core:input's new `InputDeviceTracker` follows the `InputDevice` last used, and `SceneAppLifecycleRuntime.lastInputDevice` reports it. A stick, action button or look area hidden mid-press lets go of its pointer, and one drawn smaller than `minTouchSize` (44 dp by default) still takes a touch up to that size around its centre (#878).
