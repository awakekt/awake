- **glTF `material.doubleSided` drives back-face culling.** `GltfMaterial`, `GltfMesh`, and `LoadedPrimitive` now parse glTF `material.doubleSided` (default `false` per the glTF 2.0 spec). `SceneMeshRenderer.CullMode` adds `FromMaterial` alongside a `SceneAssetResolver.defaultCullMode` hook implemented by `GltfAssetResolver`, so `FromMaterial` renders one-sided glTF primitives back-face culled (`CullMode.Back`) and two-sided ones unculled (`CullMode.None`) — including untextured and `alphaMode: MASK` materials — while omitted or explicit scene `cullMode` values keep their previous behaviour (#1587).
