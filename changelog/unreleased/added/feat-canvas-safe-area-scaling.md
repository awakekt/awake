- **A HUD fits every screen: a scaler, a safe area, and a layout that places UI on a point.** A scene's new `canvas_scaler` component, the saved form of `CanvasScaler`, works the canvas's scale out from the screen each frame: `ScaleWithScreenSize` from a reference size and a `match` between following the width and the height, `ConstantPixelSize`, or `ConstantPhysicalSize`, held by `minScale` and `maxScale` and times a player's `playerScale`. `CanvasOverlay(safeArea = CanvasInsets(…))`, `SceneCanvas(world, safeArea = …)` and `SceneAppLifecycleRuntime.canvasSafeArea` keep anchored elements clear of a notch, rounded corners or a home indicator, and an element's new `safeArea` field, `true` by default, opts a backdrop out. `PointAnchor(x, y, pivot, offsetX, offsetY)` puts any Compose content's pivot point, such as its bottom centre, on a point of its box. A scene saved before these loads and draws as it did.
