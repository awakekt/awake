- **Nine-slice frames for high-density art, hollow frames, and bars that keep their caps.** `ImageFill` and a canvas `CanvasImage` take `pixelsPerDp`, so art drawn at 2x keeps its corners and tiles at the same size on screen, and `hollow`, which leaves the centre out so a frame shows what is behind it. `drawImageFillBar(fill, value, direction)` draws a fill from any edge with both end caps whole at every value above 0, and a canvas Bar's new `style.fillImageMode` `Capped` draws its fill that way. Editor hosts get `InspectorFieldScope.imageSlices` and `ImageSlices`, the hook for editing a picture's region and slice guides on the picture itself (#880).
