- **Before/after text-clipping sheets, with lost-ink counts, in the Render Evidence comment of every font or text PR.** `./gradlew :awake:engine:render:parity:captureTextClipEvidence -Pevidence.dir=<dir>` draws 55 cases (text fields, `graphicsLayer` around text, the shadcn sidebar, font scale, a loaded font's distance-field edge, and the game canvas's text, field and fading follower) with their clips and layers and again without, on the CPU raster, and counts the pixels whose ink the clip took away (at least 8/255 missing from the render). `tools/fonts-tooling/text-evidence` composes a base and a head capture into one zoomed sheet per group, with lost ink in red and the clip or layer rect in green, and the workflow adds a "Text clipping" section to its one comment with an overview and a count table that marks the cases whose count changed. A PR that touches font data, text, clip or layer code, shaders for glyphs, the canvas or the shadcn inputs and sidebar, or any TTF or OTF, runs it; `run_local.sh <base-ref>` reproduces it locally, on a base that predates the capture too (#1079).
