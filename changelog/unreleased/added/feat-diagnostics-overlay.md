- **Diagnostics: an overlay for debug sources' rows.** `DebugRowBuffer` in `awake:core:diagnostics` lays out the title and rows of every debug source that is on in fixed slots and keeps what each slot shows between readings, so a number becomes text only when it changed and a steady reading allocates nothing. `DebugPanelSystem` in `awake:scene:canvas` shows those slots in a canvas panel ten times a second, pushing only the changed slots to their elements, and makes no panel while no source is on; `debugPanelPrefab` and `DebugPanelStyle` give the panel's prefab and its slot count, anchor, offsets and size. A scene app on the default infrastructure systems gets the panel when it registers a `DebugRegistry` service (#1218).
