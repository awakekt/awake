- **A sprite sheet or tile atlas that doesn't divide into its cells is logged once and not drawn, instead of throwing.** `SpriteRenderBatch` and `TilemapRenderBatch` threw on every frame when an image's size didn't divide into the sprite's or tilemap's columns and rows of whole pixels, which a file-backed texture first shows at run time. That sprite or tilemap is now not drawn, the sheet is logged once with its size and grid, and everything else draws. `TilemapRenderBatch` takes the atlas's name for that log line.
