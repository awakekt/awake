- **A trackpad pinch reaches the app on the web.** A browser sends a pinch as a `wheel` event with `ctrlKey` set, and `BrowserCanvas`'s wheel listener forwarded neither the modifier nor `preventDefault()`, so a pinch zoomed the whole page instead. The wheel now holds the event's Ctrl, Shift, Alt and Meta in `Input` for the frame that reads it, as desktop's Ctrl+wheel arrives (a held `Key.Ctrl` beside the scroll delta), and the listener is non-passive and cancels every wheel over the canvas so the page neither zooms nor scrolls beneath the app. macOS desktop, where GLFW reports no magnify gesture, is still open (#891).
