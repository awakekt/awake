- **Desktop text fields type what the keyboard types.** `pollGlfwTextInput` mapped held keys through a fixed US table, so Shift changed only letters (an email address typed `name2example.com`), backtick, backslash and the keypad typed nothing, AZERTY and QWERTZ users got the wrong letters, Caps Lock was ignored, and AltGr typed the bare key. Typed text now comes from GLFW's character callback, queued per window by `libawake-window`, with the layout, Shift, Caps Lock, AltGr and dead keys applied by the platform; keys drive only editing and the clipboard shortcuts, and Ctrl+Alt (AltGr on Windows) no longer copies, cuts, pastes or selects all. `GlfwWindow` adds `glfwSetCharCallback` and `glfwConsumeTypedText`, and `GlfwWindowInput.consumeTypedText()` lets a test fake typed characters.
