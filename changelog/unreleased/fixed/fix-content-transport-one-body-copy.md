- **A content fetch through Ktor no longer holds its body twice.** `KtorContentTransport` read each response with `client.get` and `bodyAsBytes`, so Ktor saved the whole body and the transport copied it out again: two copies of a content bundle on the heap for every fetch in flight. It now reads the body from the connection into the one array it returns, sized by `Content-Length` when the server sends it (#1619).
