- A camera looking straight up or down with the default +Y up, such as a canvas view's portrait camera, now has a real view and frustum. `Mat4.setLookAt` and `Frustum` build its basis with +Z for up, as the shadow cascades do for a near-vertical light. Before, its view matrix collapsed the scene onto a line and its frustum had no planes, so culling admitted every mesh with bounds: a portrait 19,900 units above a town drew 207 of the town's transparent props each frame. Now it draws none (#1621).
