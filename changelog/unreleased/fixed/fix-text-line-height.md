- **Text line boxes match a browser's, and every line of a paragraph is the same distance apart.** `line-height: normal` is now the font's line ascent and descent each rounded to a whole pixel, as Chromium rounds them, so Roboto at 11, 12, 14 and 18 px lays out at 13, 14, 17 and 22 px like the pinned Chromium oracle instead of its exact 12.89, 14.06, 16.41 and 21.09. A font file's line ascent, descent and gap come from its `OS/2` table as DirectWrite reads them (the new `UiFont.lineAscentEm`, `lineDescentEm` and `lineGapEm`). Lines advance by one whole-device-pixel line height per run rather than a fractional one with each baseline snapped, which alternated the gaps at 125% and 150% scaling; measuring and painting now share that rule (`TextLineBox`). The font's own line box never ends inside its ink: where the rounded descent falls short of the font's deepest descender at a fractional density, or of a distance-field glyph's antialiased edge, the box grows by the whole pixels it needs. An authored `TextStyle.lineHeight` below the font's own now applies to multi-line text too, and any authored line height splits its leading above and below the glyphs as CSS does, so glyphs may overlap a tight line box (#1024).
