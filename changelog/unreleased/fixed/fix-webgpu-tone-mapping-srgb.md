- **Desktop WebGPU no longer encodes colour twice.** Over wgpu-native the backend drew into the surface's first format, `BGRA8UnormSrgb`, so the GPU applied its own sRGB encode to colour the shaders had already encoded for display: frames came out washed out and a tone-mapping step drifted up to 17/255 from its CPU twin. It now draws into the UNORM counterpart when the surface offers one, as Vulkan does and as a browser canvas always does, so a WebGPU frame matches the Vulkan one and tone mapping matches its twin within 1/255 on both backends. Browser builds are unchanged. (#1011)
