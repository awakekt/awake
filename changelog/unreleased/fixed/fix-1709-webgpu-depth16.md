- WebGPU builds its shadow maps in `depth16unorm` when `WebGpuEngine.shadowDepthFormat` asks for `ShadowDepthFormat.Depth16Unorm`, as Vulkan does since #1703, and its renderer reports the format through `Renderer.shadowDepthFormat` (#1709). Every caster pipeline and retained render bundle drawing into the shadow array now declares the array's own depth format. Built with 32-bit pipelines over a 16-bit array, as before, wgpu aborts at the first cascade. At a 1024 map the 16-bit array takes 6 MiB against 12 MiB, and draws the same picture as 32-bit on lavapipe: 0 of 65,536 pixels differ. With the rasterizer bias taken off the 16-bit pipelines, 52 pixels of acne show up.
