- Vulkan on phones: a swapchain that presents suboptimally on every frame, as a landscape app on a portrait-native Android panel does, no longer throws an exception and re-queries the surface each frame. `Vulkan.vkQueuePresentKHRStatus` answers `VK_SUBOPTIMAL_KHR` and `VK_ERROR_OUT_OF_DATE_KHR` as results, and the renderer asks the surface once per swapchain whether it changed. `PresentMode.Auto`'s documentation now says what it does: FIFO (vsync), not mailbox (#1688).
