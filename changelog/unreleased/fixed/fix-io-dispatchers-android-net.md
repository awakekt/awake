- **Offload platform filesystem, resource reads, and deferred scene loading onto `ioDispatcher` and bound default WebSocket connect waits.** Added `com.awakekt.awake.core.io.ioDispatcher` (`Dispatchers.IO` on Desktop/Android/iOS, `Dispatchers.Default` on WasmJs), wrapped blocking `AndroidFileSystem`, `NioFileSystem`, `IosFileSystem`, and `readResourceBytes` calls in `withContext(Dispatchers.IO)`, moved `SceneRouterRuntime`'s default deferred-route load scope from `Dispatchers.Default` to `ioDispatcher`, and installed a 10 s `HttpTimeout.connectTimeoutMillis` on `KtorWebSocketTransport`'s default client.
