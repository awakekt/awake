- `IndexedAssetSource` holds a fetched content bundle once and copies each member out only as it is cached or read, and fetches one bundle at a time by default, so a world's bundles no longer fill the heap on entry. An 80 MiB bundle of 64 members, written to a cache, peaks at 83 MiB of retained heap, down from 209 MiB (336 MiB sealed), and four 20 MiB bundles prefetched together at 23 MiB, down from 116 MiB. `BundleMemoryLimits`, a new parameter of the constructor and of `open`, sets how many bundles are fetched at once and how many bytes of members are kept without a cache (#1619).
