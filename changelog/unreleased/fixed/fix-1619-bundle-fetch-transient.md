- `IndexedAssetSource` holds a fetched content bundle once and copies each member out only as it is cached or read, and fetches two bundles at a time by default instead of up to six, so a world's bundles no longer fill the heap on entry. An 80 MiB bundle of 64 members, written to a cache, peaks at 83 MiB of retained heap, down from 209 MiB (336 MiB sealed). `BundleMemoryLimits`, a new parameter of the constructor and of `open`, sets how many bundles are fetched at once (a phone sets 1) and how many bytes of members are kept without a cache (#1619).
