- **A paused app no longer reads as running.** `SceneAppLifecycleRuntime.fps` averaged the last frames' times with no clock, so while an Android app sat in the background (its render thread stopped, as it should) the value stayed at the last foreground rate. A consumer build reported 66 fps for five minutes. The runtime now forgets its recorded frames on `pause()`: `fps`, `averageFrameTimeMs` and the scene's frame stats read 0 until frames arrive again, and the new `isPaused` says which. `FrameStats.reset()` is the new call that does it. `docs/architecture/architecture.md` now describes what stops when an Android app goes to the background, and that the host calls nothing.
