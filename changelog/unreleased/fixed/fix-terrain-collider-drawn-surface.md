- A terrain collider lies on the ground the clipmap draws (#1768). `TerrainColliderSystem` built the Jolt heightfield from the raw heightmap samples, which splits each quad once along its diagonal, while level 0 of the clipmap draws it as finer cells displaced by the bilinear height. On a twisted quad the two differ by up to `|a+d-b-c|/4`, and a character stood up to 1.5 m above or under the drawn slope. The collider is now built at level 0's spacing from bilinearly upsampled samples, with the same diagonal as the drawn mesh, on every host, a game server included. New in `awake:asset:terrain`: `Heightmap.clipmapColliderFactor` and `Heightmap.upsampledSamples`. A 513x513 map at 4 m spacing builds a 1025x1025 collider.
