- **A turned picture drawn in place of its graphics layer samples the same pixels on every GPU.** A layer holding only pictures skips its offscreen pass and draws them straight into the parent, but the picture's image carried a mip chain the layer's one-level target does not, so its colour followed the driver's level-of-detail estimate for the turn: an RTX drew a 4x2 picture turned 30 degrees differently from its layer while lavapipe drew both alike. A picture the layer holds at no less than its image's size now samples only the image's full-size level, as the layer would; a picture the layer shrinks keeps its mips.
