- **A cancelled read in `awake:core:io` or `awake:project` stops instead of reporting a failure.** `FunctionResourceReader.read`, `FileSystem.read`/`write`, `InMemoryFileSystem.transaction`, `FileSystemContentCache.put`, `IndexedAssetSource.read`, `BundleCipher.seal` and `TokenAuthorizedFetch` wrapped suspend calls in `runCatching`, which caught the coroutine's cancellation too. They now rethrow it, through the new `suspendRunCatching` in `awake:core:io`, a `runCatching` that rethrows `CancellationException`. On desktop, a `FileSystem.transaction` cancelled while it applies its changes now puts the files back before the cancellation goes on, as a failed one already did (#1321).
