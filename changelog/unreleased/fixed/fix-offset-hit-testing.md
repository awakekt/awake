- **A node moved by `Modifier.offset` shows its cursor, takes focus and scrolls where it is drawn.** A cursor request, a focus target and a scroll area were judged against the node's own box, which an offset leaves in place, so an offset handle showed its resize cursor, took a press and caught the wheel at the place it had left, and hid whatever lay under that place. They now follow the modifier chain as a click target already did: one declared after an offset is where the offset put it, one declared before stays with the node. A padding outside them still leaves the node's whole box, as before.
