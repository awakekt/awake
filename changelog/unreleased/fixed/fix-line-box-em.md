- **Text lines are no longer too loose at fractional display scales.** The line box was built from the glyph size rounded to whole device pixels, so 14 sp text at 125% advanced 23 px per line instead of 21. It is now built from the size as authored, as a browser does, while the glyphs are still drawn at whole device pixels; whole scales such as 100%, 150% and 200% are unchanged. (#1072)
