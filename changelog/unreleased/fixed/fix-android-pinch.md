- Two fingers on an Android touch screen now pinch: the Android bridge turns the distance between the first two fingers into `Input.addPinch` steps about their midpoint, so `InputSnapshot.pinch` is set and the built-in cameras zoom. The math is `TouchPinch` in `awake:core:input`, for any touch bridge whose platform reports no pinch. While two or more fingers are down, `CameraSystem` and `CameraInputProcessor` take no orbit or pan drag, so the first finger moving with the pinch no longer turns the camera; `InputSnapshot.isMultiTouch` and `GameplayInput.isMultiTouch` say when that is.
