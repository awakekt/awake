- **A wrapping canvas row sized to fit its children stays on one line at fractional scales.** `FlexBox` compared each child's rounded pixel width against the box with no tolerance, so at a scale such as 0.75, 1.5 or 1.75 the rounding added up and a row of slots authored to fill its bar exactly pushed its last slot onto a new line. When the density is not whole, a line may now run up to one pixel per child past the box, the most rounding can add; the children still draw on whole pixels, a row over by more still wraps, and whole densities are unchanged.
