- **A corner-anchored heightmap stays where it is through an edit.** `Heightmap.mutableCopy()` and `MutableHeightmap.snapshot()` left out the map's `GridOrigin`, so the copy fell back to centred: a corner-anchored terrain moved half a map on the first edit made through a copy, such as an editor's sculpt stroke, and `heightAtWorld` read the wrong place. Both now keep it, and `MutableHeightmap` has an `origin`, centred by default.
