- **Tone-only and bloom-only post-processing no longer sample the target they draw into on Vulkan.** A stack whose last step was tone mapping (an explicit tone-only stack, or bloom's Passthrough composite on display-ready colour) read `ImagePassSource.DestinationSnapshot` while writing the active target. WebGPU copies the snapshot, but Vulkan samples the image in place, so that step read the image it was drawing into, and validation reported `VUID-VkDescriptorImageInfo-imageLayout-00344`. `PostProcessingStack.buildChain` now copies the target's colour into `TRANSIENT_SCENE_COPY_ID` (in the target's own format, so HDR stays HDR) and the last tone-mapping step reads that copy, as FXAA already did after #1007.
