- **A held animation pose waits its interval, not a frame longer (#1423).** `AnimationSystem` tested a far model's hold before counting the frame's delta toward it and dropped the overshoot, so every hold ran one frame long: an interval shorter than a frame still posed only every other frame, and just past `near` the pose rate fell to about half. The hold now counts the delta first and carries the remainder, so an interval below a frame poses every frame and the pose rate averages `1 / interval`. The clip's clock is unchanged while a pose is held.
