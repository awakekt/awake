- **Glyphs are no longer cut off by clips and layers sized to the text box.** A text field now clips its text at its padding box, as CSS does, keeping the tight clip only on a side its single line is scrolled past (new `DrawScope.clippedWithMargin`), and `ShadcnSidebar` clips before its padding, so an accent's ring, a leading `j`'s tail or a descender that reaches past the text box keeps its ink. A `graphicsLayer`'s render target grows to what its content paints instead of cutting it at the node, so text no longer loses its edges when a fade or a press scale starts. Text and text fields paint at the font scale they were measured at, a loaded font's distance-field fringe fades out inside each glyph's quad instead of ending at a hard edge, and the GPU scissor for a fractional clip covers the pixels whose centres the clip contains.
