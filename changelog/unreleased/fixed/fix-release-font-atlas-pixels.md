- **A UI font's atlas pixels no longer stay on the heap after the atlas is uploaded.** `UiFonts.weightedSans()` kept its combined atlas and the seven face atlases it is stacked from, about 31 MB, for as long as the font lived, beside the texture a renderer had made from them. `UiFont.releaseAtlasPixels()` lets a font that can build its pixels again drop them, and the Vulkan and WebGPU renderers and `WorldTextBatch` call it once the atlas is in a texture. The packed, weighted, numeric and fallback fonts release theirs and build the same pixels again on the next read of `atlasPixelsRgba`; a font whose pixels are its only copy keeps them (#1619).
