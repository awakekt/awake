- **A reused Compose node declared without content clears its previous children, layers, and slots.** `Composer.node` only truncated stale children, layers, and `remember` slots when `content != null`, so an unkeyed childless container such as `Box(Modifier.fillMaxSize())` inserted ahead of a populated `Box { ... }` reused slot `0` and kept painting the previous frame's children at the new bounds alongside the freshly created second `Box`. Reusing a node with `content == null` now drops all children and layers and forgets its remembered slots in descendant-first order.
