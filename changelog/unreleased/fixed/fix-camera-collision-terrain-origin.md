- **Camera collision samples offset terrains at their world placement and never collapses look-at onto world up.** `SceneCameraCollisionProbes` and `TerrainClipmapSystem` read each `TerrainComponent` entity's heightmap as though it sat at the world origin even when its `Transform` placed it elsewhere in a multi-terrain scene, so hills on an adjoining terrain pulled a follow camera onto its pivot and lifted it straight above it. That made `center - eye` parallel to world up `(0, 1, 0)` and left `Lens`'s look-at matrix singular, blacking out the 3D view while piling followed canvas elements onto the screen centre. Both now offset terrain lookups by the entity's world translation (including parented prefab hierarchies before `TransformSystem` runs), `CameraCollisionSolver` keeps a minimum offset from the pivot and off the vertical axis when lifting above ground so `viewProjection` stays invertible, and `SceneCanvasProjector` returns `null` when a camera's look-at basis is degenerate.
