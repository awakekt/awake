- `TransformSystem` no longer visits every `StaticTransform` entity each frame: a frame in which no static joined, left, swapped or reset its transform skips them, and the moving entities' family is kept instead of built anew. A still town of 10,000 marked props with 10 movers costs about 0.7 µs and 0 B a frame on the JVM instead of 16 µs and 688 B, and 2 µs instead of 48 µs on wasm. `Family1.changes` and `Family2.changes` count a family's joins, leaves and component swaps (#1621).
