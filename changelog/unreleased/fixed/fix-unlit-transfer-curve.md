- **Unlit draws keep their texel under distance fog.** `depthFogContentFeature` blends fog over every pixel the scene depth holds, after the geometry, so it also painted over `PbrMaterial.unlit` draws, which promise to skip fog. With a pale fog an unlit skin came out a few levels brighter than its texture, most on its darker channels. A draw can now ask to be drawn over the frame's coverings (`GpuDrawRequest.overCoverings`), and the scene sets it for every unlit material, static or skinned. On Vulkan and WebGPU the engines record those draws in `OverCoveringsRenderFeature`, after the `ContentPaint.AfterGeometry` features, followed by the wireframe edges, the transparent draws and the debug lines, so a transparent surface in front of an unlit one still blends over it. Lit opaque draws are fogged as before; transparent draws now blend over the fogged frame instead of being covered by the fog pass.
