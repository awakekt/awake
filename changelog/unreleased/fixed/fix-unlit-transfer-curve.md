- **Unlit draws keep their texel under distance fog.** `depthFogContentFeature` blends fog over every pixel the scene depth holds, after the geometry, so it also painted over `PbrMaterial.unlit` draws, which promise to skip fog. With a pale fog an unlit skin came out a few levels brighter than its texture, most on its darker channels. A draw can now ask to be drawn over the frame's coverings (`GpuDrawRequest.overCoverings`, recorded by `OverCoveringsRenderFeature` after the `ContentPaint.AfterGeometry` features on Vulkan and WebGPU), and the scene sets it for every unlit material, static or skinned. Lit draws are fogged as before.
