- **A stroked closed circle, ellipse or arc path draws as an outline instead of a filled disc.** A full arc ends a float's rounding away from where it began, so closing it added a segment a few millionths of a pixel long, and that segment's direction was noise. Offsetting it folded the stroke's inner edge back on itself, so the hole was lost and the whole shape filled. The stroker now merges points less than a thousandth of a pixel apart before it offsets a contour, so zero-length segments, a repeated point included, join their neighbours with no fill and no spike. This holds at any stroke width, for every join and on every backend, since they all stroke through `strokeToFillPath`.
