- **A skinned mesh is lit by the scene's sun.** A skinned mesh drawn on its own (`skinned_textured` and the non-instanced `skinned` shader) lit every pose with a fixed sun, so turning the scene's `Light` or changing its colour left the mesh as it was. Both shaders now take the sun's direction and colour from the skinned uniform block, which already carried them for the debug views; a scene without a light gets the default sun, which points where the fixed one did. The sun's colour tints only its own light: in a scene lit only by point lights, which these shaders do not take, a skinned mesh keeps its ambient share instead of the fixed sun.
