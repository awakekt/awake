- **A wrapping `FlexBox` sizes its `Text` items by their real wrapped height.** `Text` reported no intrinsic height, so a FlexBox column gave every label none, a wrapping row planned its lines as if they held only the fixed-size items, and `alignContent` placed and stretched lines from those planned heights, so rows overlapped or were clipped. `Text` now answers its min and max intrinsic height at a width with the height it measures there, line breaks at the width a parent planned from its intrinsic width no longer push the last word onto a second line, and `FlexBox` plans each line at its items' final main sizes, places lines by what its items measured, shares a stretch's leftover pixels instead of dropping them, and reports intrinsics for its direction and wrap instead of summing every child's width.
