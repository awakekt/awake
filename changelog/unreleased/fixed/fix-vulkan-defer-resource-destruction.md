- **Freeing a mesh or material no longer races the frames that draw with it on Vulkan.** `Mesh.destroy()` and `Material.destroy()` freed their buffers, memory and descriptor sets at once, so a scene swap destroyed the old scene's resources while submitted frames still read them: undefined behaviour, reported by the validation layer as `VUID-vkDestroyBuffer-buffer-00922` on every reload. They now first wait for the frames the renderer has submitted, swapchain and offscreen alike, through `GraphicsDevice.awaitSubmittedFrames`, which the renderer installs and removes. A destroy can now wait for up to two frames. `DynamicMesh`, `LineMesh` and textures are not covered, and neither are the objects the validation layer reports as leaked at device destruction.
