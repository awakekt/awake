- Two fingers on a touch screen in a browser now pinch: the browser's touch bridge feeds the fingers still down after each touch event to `TouchPinch`, so `InputSnapshot.pinch` is set and the built-in cameras zoom, as on Android and iOS. Safari on a touch screen also sends its own gesture events for those fingers; a gesture with two fingers down is left to the touches, so the pinch is counted once. A trackpad pinch in Safari, which has no touches, still pinches through its gesture events.
