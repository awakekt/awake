- **Particle emitters spawned at runtime simulate and draw when the scene document has none of its own (#1389).** `ParticlesCapability` planned `ParticleContentSystem` and `ParticleSystem` only for a document that had a `particle_emitter`, so emitters in prefabs spawned later through `SceneHostServices.spawn` were bound but never emitted, moved or drew. The systems are now planned whenever the scene has a renderer; with nothing to emit, each frame is an empty query. A document no longer needs a placeholder emitter with `spawnRate: 0` to make spawned ones work.
