- **A Vulkan backend whose start-up is cancelled part way releases what it built**, as a failed one already did, and the cancellation goes on instead of being reported as a start-up failure. `GraphicsEngine.create` and `SaveScheduler` keep treating a cancellation their own work raised, such as a backend's or a save writer's own timeout, as a failure, and stop only when their own coroutine is cancelled; a `SaveScheduler` closed mid-write still answers that batch's callback with `false` (#1321).
