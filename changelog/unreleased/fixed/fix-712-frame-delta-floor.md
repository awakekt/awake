- **A browser game no longer stops after its first frame.** A frame's delta was capped at `MAX_FRAME_DELTA_SECONDS` but never floored at 0. In the browser, the first `requestAnimationFrame` timestamp can read earlier than the `performance.now()` taken before it, so the first delta could be negative, `AnimationPlayer` rejected it ("Animation delta must be non-negative.") and the frame loop stopped. Every host now clamps through `clampFrameDelta`, to `0..MAX_FRAME_DELTA_SECONDS`.
