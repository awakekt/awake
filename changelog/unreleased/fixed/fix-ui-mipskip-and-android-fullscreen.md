- **Compose UI images upload at full resolution regardless of `Renderer.textureMipSkip`.** `ImageTextures` now clears `textureMipSkip` around UI bitmap uploads and restores it afterward, honoring the `#1660` contract so low-quality texture settings never quarter-size UI icons or sprites on Vulkan or WebGPU.
- **Android fullscreen checks status and navigation bar visibility individually.** `ActivitySystemBars` now checks `WindowInsets.Type.statusBars()` and `navigationBars()` instead of `systemBars()` (and hides/shows those two masks), avoiding the `captionBar()` false-negative that locked Android fullscreen active on phones.
