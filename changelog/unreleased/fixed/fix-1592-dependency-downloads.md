- A dependency or plugin download that Maven Central or the Plugin Portal answers with a 429, a 5xx or a timeout is retried for about a minute, not three seconds (#1592). Gradle's default of three attempts gave up inside Maven Central's rate limiting, which failed five CI runs on 16-17 Nov before they built anything. `gradle.properties` and `build-logic/gradle.properties` raise the attempts to six and the first wait to 2 s.
