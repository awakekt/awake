- **A canvas window moved partly off the canvas keeps its size.** An anchored canvas element was placed by applying its offset as padding, so a movable window, one whose move handle keeps only its title bar on the canvas, was measured in the space left before the edge once dragged past it, and squashed (a 240×464 window in a private consumer pack became 170×20 in a bottom corner). `CanvasPlacement.AnchoredElement` now measures the element at its own size, aligned to its anchor, so a change of position never changes its size; the part past the edge is only clipped.
