- **Speed-matched locomotion playback.** `locomotion_animation` gains optional `walkReferenceSpeed` and `runReferenceSpeed` — the ground speed its walk or run cycle was authored for. When set, `LocomotionAnimationSystem` scales that clip's playback by the mover's smoothed ground speed over the reference, so the stride matches the distance covered and the feet plant instead of sliding; a mover whose speed differs from the clip's authored cadence no longer foot-skates. Left unset (the default), clips play at their authored rate, as before (#1226).
