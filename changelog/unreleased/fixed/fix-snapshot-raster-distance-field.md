- **UI snapshots draw distance-field text as sharp as the GPU does.** The CPU raster behind every UI baseline took half the glyph shader's screen-pixel range (only the horizontal scale, halved) and averaged several samples a pixel when a glyph was drawn smaller than its atlas cell, so text above the 24 px pixel-glyph limit, and text under a display scale, came out soft and grey, with grey marks where channels disagree inside `6`, `9` and `e`. It now computes the shader's own range, median, coverage ramp and gamma from one bilinear sample at each pixel centre the quad covers, checked against the shader through the ASL evaluator, and agrees with headless Vulkan to a mean of 0.5 luminance levels at 28-96 px and scales 1 and 1.5, where it was 10-33 before. `AslEvaluator.traceFragment` takes `letOverrides`, to supply a texture read or derivative the evaluator cannot compute. Two baselines moved: `pixel-text-large` and `scene-canvas-font-file`.
