- Fitting shadow cascades to their casters on a backend that cannot clamp shadow depth no longer tests every caster against every cascade's casting planes each frame: a caster's lowest depth is measured first, and only one below the cascade's near plane meets the planes, in one pass over the casters for all cascades. A moving frame over 3,000 shadowed props plans in about 281 µs instead of 347 µs on the JVM (#1621).
