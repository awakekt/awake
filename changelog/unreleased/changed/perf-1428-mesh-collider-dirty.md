- **`MeshColliderSystem` visits only colliders still waiting for a body (#1428).** It walked every `MeshCollider` and `ConvexHullCollider` entity on every fixed step and looked up its `PhysicsBody` by class, even when every body was long built. Each collider kind is now a maintained family of `Transform` and the collider that excludes `PhysicsBody`. An entity joins it when it gains a collider, or loses its body, and leaves it when its body is added. A step with nothing to build reads two sizes.
