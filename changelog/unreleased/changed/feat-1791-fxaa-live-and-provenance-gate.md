- **FXAA turns on and off from the next frame.** A settings screen that changes `RenderQualitySettings.antiAliasing` between `Off` and `Fxaa` no longer waits for a restart: the engine applies it before the next frame through the new `Renderer.switchAntiAliasing`, on a single-sampled scene pass, on Vulkan and WebGPU. A change to or from an MSAA mode still rebuilds the scene's attachments and pipelines, so it applies on the next start. `awakeVerify` also gains a provenance gate: a shader that carries a name listed in `docs/reference/third-party-names.json` (FXAA, SMAA, Kawase, the dual-filter blur, PBR Neutral) fails without a `source-provenance.json` entry that names it, or a pending review owned by a tracker.
