- **Scene loads run under the scene's or the app's coroutine scope.** `AssetSourceTextureResolver` and a project's spawned glTF models load in a child of `SceneSession.coroutineScope`, and a `SceneRouterRuntime`'s deferred routes in a child of the app's `AppCoroutineScope`, so their failures reach the app's error handler and disposing the scene or the app stops them. `SceneAssetResolver.attach(sessionScope)` is new: the session calls it before its first frame. An `AssetSourceTextureResolver` used outside a scene session takes the scope its loads run under, `AssetSourceTextureResolver(files, scope, cooked)`; one that has neither throws when it is first asked for a texture (#1321).
