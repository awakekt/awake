- `Modifier.border` on a uniformly rounded shape draws one analytic `RoundedQuad` with a new `strokeWidth`: two triangles where the tessellated ring was about a thousand. Its coverage is a pixel-wide box filter of each edge, solid on a pixel-aligned 1 px edge. Against 8 x 8 supersampled coverage it is closer than the ring, from 0.53 to 0.12 mean channel steps for a 1 px border at radius 8. A vector HUD fixture's first frame drops from 109,136 to 12,108 triangles and from 7.95 MB to 0.90 MB uploaded. Square borders keep the tessellated ring, which is exact on their corners. The rounded-quad vertex format gains a stroke width, at 17 floats, and `DrawScope.drawRoundedRectBorder` draws such a border directly. Rounded-border pixels in the CPU baselines moved by up to a few channel steps at their corners (#1545).
