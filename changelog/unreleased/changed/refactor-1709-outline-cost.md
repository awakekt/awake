- **Outlines cost what they outline.** The outline overlay drew a full-screen pass reading 34 mask depth samples per pixel on every frame that had any mask pass, including frames where only an unlit mesh, which joins the covering-exempt mask layer, was drawn and nothing was outlined. It now draws nothing unless a mask layer has an `OutlineStyle` that `draws` (at least `OUTLINE_MIN_WIDTH_PIXELS` wide and not transparent), takes no samples for a layer with no such style, and is scissored to the masked draws' screen bounds (`GpuSubPass.screenBounds`), padded by the outline width. On a plan with no scene-depth pass, the mask target follows the scene's size after the render scale (`MaskTargetSizing`, at most the old 2048 edge) instead of a fixed 2048 square of three D32 layers: 15.9 MB instead of 50.3 MB at 1093x480. With a scene-depth pass it keeps that target's size, because fog compares the two. `Renderer.maskTargetBytes` reports it. (#1709)
