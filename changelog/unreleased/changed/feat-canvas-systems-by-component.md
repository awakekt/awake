- **The canvas draws and reads its controls by component.** The overlay draws each element by its widget component, or its words or a plain box when it has none, and routes taps by whether it has a `CanvasButtonWidget` or a pressable `CanvasInput`; `CanvasActionSystem` reads joysticks, buttons and look areas as `family<CanvasJoystickWidget, CanvasPointerState>()` and the like. Nothing branches on an element's `kind` any more, so a widget added later needs a view and no change to the rest. What is drawn and what a played project does are unchanged (#1088).
