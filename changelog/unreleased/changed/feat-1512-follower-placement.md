- Canvas followers are planned and placed while placing, not while composing: with `ComposeHost.skipUnchangedComposition` and `skipUnchangedMeasure` on, a frame whose followed nodes only move places and draws its followers without composing or measuring, and composes again, in the same frame, only when which followers are drawn, or one's size, scale, density or pin, changes (#1512). A world canvas of 200 name plates and a minimap draws a moving frame in 1.3 ms and 378 KB on desktop, against 4.7 ms and 2,874 KB when it composed every frame. A camera view repaints itself each frame. `FrameClock.frame` counts the host's frames.
