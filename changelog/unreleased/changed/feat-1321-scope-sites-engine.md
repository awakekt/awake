- **The engine's start-up, the browser canvas's start-up and WebGPU's GPU timestamp readbacks run under the app's coroutine scope.** `GraphicsEngine.create` starts the backend in a child of the app's `AppCoroutineScope`, kept as the engine's protected `coroutineScope` for a backend's background work and cancelled by `dispose()`, and a start-up cancelled that way is no longer reported as a failure. `WindowLifecycle.services` is new: a `GraphicsEngine` answers with its app's services, and `runBrowserCanvas` starts its backend in a child of that app's scope, on the browser's main dispatcher, instead of a `MainScope()`. The WebGPU renderer reads its GPU timestamps back in a child of the engine's scope, and a cancelled readback is no longer turned into a lost frame time (#1321).
