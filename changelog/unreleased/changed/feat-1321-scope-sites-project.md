- **A played project's background work runs under the scene session's scope.** Scene switch loads, `terrain` surface resolving and `paged_terrain` cell reads run in children of `SceneSession.coroutineScope`, so their failures reach the app's error handler and disposing the scene stops them. `SceneHostServices.withCoroutineScope(scope)` passes that scope to `sceneSystemsFor`, and `LoadedProject.sceneSystems` takes it as `coroutineScope`; `runProject` passes the session's own. A host that builds the systems of a scene with a drawn `terrain` or any `paged_terrain` and passes no scope now gets an `IllegalStateException` naming `withCoroutineScope`, where before each system made a scope nobody owned. `Strings.removeSourceSoon(source)` is new: it takes a source out without suspending or starting a coroutine, and `LoadedProject.close` removes the project's tables from the app's `Strings` with it (#1321).
