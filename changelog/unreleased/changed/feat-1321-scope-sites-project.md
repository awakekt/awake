- **A played project's background work runs under the scene session's scope.** Scene switch loads, `terrain` surface resolving and `paged_terrain` cell reads run in children of `SceneSession.coroutineScope`, so their failures reach the app's error handler and disposing the scene stops them; `runProject` passes the session's scope. `SceneHostServices.withCoroutineScope(scope)` is new and passes a host's scope to `sceneSystemsFor`, and `LoadedProject.sceneSystems` takes it as `coroutineScope`. `Strings.removeSourceSoon(source)` is new: it takes a source out without suspending or starting a coroutine, and `LoadedProject.close` removes the project's tables from the app's `Strings` with it (#1321).
- **Deprecated: building a scene's systems without a scope.** `LoadedProject.sceneSystems(input, renderer, audio, fixedStep)` without `coroutineScope` is deprecated, and a host whose `SceneHostServices` has no scope logs a warning once: their systems' background work falls back to a scope nobody owns, which nothing cancels with the scene. To migrate, pass the scene session's scope: `project.sceneSystems(input, coroutineScope = session.coroutineScope)`, or `services.withCoroutineScope(session.coroutineScope)` before `sceneSystemsFor`. The fallback goes with the deprecated overload (#1321).
- **`AssetLoads` runs under its owner's scope.** `AssetLoads(scope, context, read)` takes the scope a load nobody gave a scope of its own runs in, under a job of the loads' own that `close()` cancels, instead of a scope it built itself; `audioClipLoads(assets, scope, ...)` and `particleImageLoads(assets, scope, ...)` take it too, and a played project passes its scene session's (#1321).
