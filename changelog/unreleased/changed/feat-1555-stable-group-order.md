- Folded instance groups keep their place in the scene's draw list from frame to frame. Before, a group followed its first copy this frame, so culling that copy moved the group and every draw after it, and a backend that keys per-draw buffers by position re-uploaded them all. Groups are now ordered by a batch key kept across frames (mesh, material, per-instance tinting, cull mode, alpha mode). A group that disappears frees its place, and a returning or new one comes after the rest (#1555).
