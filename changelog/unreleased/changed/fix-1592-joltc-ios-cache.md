- The iOS native action caches the JoltC and JoltPhysics static libraries, as it already caches MoltenVK, keyed on the JoltC submodule commit and on the build script and CMake helper that configure it (#1592). On a hit the CMake build is skipped. Hit or miss, both slices are checked: present, arm64, and for the right platform (iOS device or simulator). Only a verified build is saved. On v0.54.0 the build took 1 min 46 s in Consumer and ran again in Release; `scripts/ios-native-cache.test.sh` holds the restore, build, verify and save order in CI's Release scripts job.
