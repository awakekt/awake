- **Small text spaces evenly and keeps its crossbars solid.** Exact-size pixel glyphs now come in four subpixel variants, and a run draws each glyph as the variant nearest its pen's fraction of a pixel, with its main stem still on the pixel grid and the rest of the glyph where layout puts it, so gaps no longer jump by up to a pixel between letters: the error in the gaps of "0123456789" at 11 to 14 px falls from 0.51, 0.41, 0.41 and 0.44 px rms (up to 0.82) to 0.13, 0.05, 0.20 and 0.23 (up to 0.44), and "HP 87/100" at 15 px leaves 3 clear pixels after the 1 and 1 between the zeros, as Chromium does, where it left 4 and 0. Only a glyph whose straight stem is at least 45% of its height is pinned, so a round glyph such as a zero is no longer widened by one. The main horizontal bars of a glyph, such as the crossbar of an `e` and the bars of `H`, `A`, `F`, `E` and `B`, are found from its outline and snapped to whole rows through the vertical hinting map, so each is one solid row instead of two grey ones, keeping the baseline, x-height and cap-height snaps. A size's page holds every variant, about 148 KB of atlas per font weight and size for the 102 built-in glyphs (was 41 KB) and about 11 ms to build (was 3 ms), and is byte-identical on every target. `PixelGlyphs.glyph` gains an overload taking the subpixel step, `PixelGlyph` gains `subpixelSteps` and loses `anchorX`, which the variants replace (#1005).
