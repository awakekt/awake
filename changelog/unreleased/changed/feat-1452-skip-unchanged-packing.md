- **A static draw's uniform block is packed once, not every frame (#1452).** While the camera, lights, shadow cascades and fog hold, a draw packed from the same inputs as the frame before keeps the block its uniform slot already holds, on WebGPU, instead of being packed again only to find nothing to upload. `FrameUniformInputs` and `DrawUniformInputs` in `render:passes` tell a backend when that is so. On a still static scene of 1,300 draws this cut the frame's draw preparation by about a third.
