- **A scaled Vulkan frame writes the screen image once.** With a render scale below 1, the upscale and the UI now draw in one render pass on the swapchain image. Before, they took three native-size passes: a clear that ended at once, the upscale on a reload, and the UI on another reload. On a phone's tile-based GPU that wrote the whole screen image three times and read it back twice a frame. On lavapipe a scaled frame takes 2 render passes instead of 4, and the presented frame is byte-for-byte the same. `FrameGpuTiming.upscaleAndUiMs` reads the two parts together, since on a tile-based GPU the timestamp between them, written inside one pass, splits them unreliably.
