- **Lit surfaces keep their colour instead of washing out to grey.** Every lit shader (`lit_shadow`, `textured` and their instanced and skinned variants, on Vulkan and WebGPU) shows its colour through one shared transform: an exposure, Khronos PBR Neutral, then the gamma encode. This replaces per-channel Reinhard, which compressed bright channels more than dim ones and turned an amber cube khaki. `lit_shadow` now treats light colour as reflectance, as `textured` already did, so a light of intensity 1 lights a white face to near white instead of mid-grey. Scenes that raised intensities to make up for this need about a third of the old value. The engine showcase's point lights go from 6 to 2. A new `tone_mapping` scene component sets `exposure` (default 1), and the curve deepens surfaces only the ambient reaches.
