- **One text coverage rule for every glyph path, held to Chromium in both polarities.** Exact-size pixel glyphs and distance-field text now turn coverage into alpha by the same rule, on Vulkan, WebGPU and the CPU snapshot raster: Skia's mask gamma with Chromium's Linux constants (gamma 1.2, contrast 0.2), which assumes the ground is the text colour's opposite. It replaces gamma 1.8 with contrast 1 for pixel glyphs and `pow(coverage, 1 / 1.45)` for the distance field. Light text on dark grounds no longer draws heavier than dark text on light ones: against Chromium rendering the same Roboto label from 9 to 48 px, ink is now within 6% in both polarities (light-on-dark small text was 1.16 to 1.29 times Chromium's). Text keeps its weight across the 24 px pixel-glyph limit, where ink per em used to jump 9 to 10%. Text with a light colour, and text larger than 24 px or scaled, draws with lighter edges; 41 component baselines and the four two-font GPU baselines are re-recorded. The glyph specimen gains light-on-dark sheets, `TextPolarityWeightTest` measures against Chromium references written by `tools/fonts-tooling/chromium_polarity_reference.cjs`, and the UI guide documents the rule and how text colour and custom shaders interact with it (#1003, #1064).
