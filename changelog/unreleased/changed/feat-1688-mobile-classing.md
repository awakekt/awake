- Render quality on phones: on Android and iOS, Vulkan now classes a GPU its driver reports as integrated (or virtual, or other) as `GpuDeviceClass.Mobile`, and `RenderQuality.Auto`'s Mobile budget is a 720-pixel short edge (was 900). A 2400 by 1080 phone that drew the full 1080 lines as `Integrated` now draws 720 under Auto, about 2.25 times fewer pixels; browsers already classed as Mobile go from 900 to 720 too. Mobile also brings the Mobile defaults elsewhere: `EffectQuality.Low` under Auto, and `TextureSettings`' Medium quality and 4x anisotropy. Desktop classing is unchanged. To get the previous budgets back, pass your own: `RenderQualityBudgets(autoShortEdge = RenderQualityBudgets.Default.autoShortEdge + (GpuDeviceClass.Mobile to 1080))` keeps a phone at its full 1080 lines, and `... to 900` keeps the old Mobile budget (#1688).
