- **`SpatialGrid` moves to `com.awakekt.awake.core:spatial` (`com.awakekt.awake.core.spatial`), with `BoundedVolume` in `com.awakekt.awake.core:math`.** `SpatialGrid` and `DEFAULT_SPATIAL_CELL_SIZE` now live in `:awake:core:spatial`, depending only on `:awake:core:math`, so spatial AABB, range, frustum and volume queries can be used without the scene or render modules. `BoundedVolume` in `:awake:core:math` (`ShadowCasterVolume` implements it) backs a general `SpatialGrid.queryVolume(volume, out)`. `:awake:scene:scene3d` keeps `SpatialIndex` and `SpatialIndexSystem` alongside deprecated source-compatible `SpatialGrid`, `DEFAULT_SPATIAL_CELL_SIZE` and `SpatialGrid.queryShadowCasters` forwards; precompiled JVM consumers of the old `com.awakekt.awake.scene.rendering.spatial.SpatialGrid` class name recompile against `:awake:core:spatial` (#1025).
