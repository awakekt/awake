- WebGPU gives each draw its uniform slot and instance buffers by what the draw is (its mesh and material, and among draws sharing both, its order) instead of by its position in the frame's draw list. Culling or reordering a draw earlier in the list no longer makes every later draw re-pack its uniform block and re-upload its instances. In a 3,000-renderer scene that culls copies every frame, instance uploads fall from about 124 KB to under 1 KB a frame. A key the previous batch did not draw gives its slots back when the next begins, so slots in use never exceed two consecutive batches' draws (#1555).
