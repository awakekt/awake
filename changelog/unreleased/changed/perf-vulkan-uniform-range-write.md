- **Vulkan writes a frame's changed uniform slots without copying them first.** A slot block's flush copied its dirty range out of the staged array into a new one before handing it to the driver, which allocated that range every frame: in a populated 3D scene a consumer measured about 160 KB a frame on the frame thread, the largest Vulkan share of its garbage. `VulkanBuffers.writeBufferMemoryFloats(device, memory, offset, data, dataOffset, count)` writes part of an array in place, and the flush now writes the staged range through it. Desktop and Android go through the same one-copy JNI path as the whole-array write; iOS copies from the offset.
