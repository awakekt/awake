- **A decoded terrain holds its heights in one `FloatArray`.** `SceneTerrain.samples` decoded into a `List<Float>` of boxed floats, kept for as long as the scene document stayed loaded: 20.4 bytes a height, about 14 MiB for a 707,000-height region in a populated scene a consumer measured on its way to Android's heap limit (#1753). The samples now decode, and export, into one `FloatArray` behind the same `List<Float>` type, 4 bytes a height. A scene's JSON, its exported schema and `SceneTerrain`'s signatures are unchanged, and the decoded terrain equals one built from a plain list of the same heights.
