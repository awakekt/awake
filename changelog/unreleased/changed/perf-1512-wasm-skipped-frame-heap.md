- `Box` places its children without allocating on Kotlin/Wasm (#1512). The standard `Alignment`s, and the alignment `Box` builds from a horizontal and a vertical one, are objects rather than lambdas through the SAM constructor, whose Wasm wrapper boxed both `IntSize`s and the `IntOffset` result on every child placed. The canvas's `OnPlacedListener`s are objects too, for the same reason, and `Constraints` reads its maximums without boxing.
