- **Compose can replay a subtree's last draw output instead of drawing it.** `ComposeHost.retainUnchangedDraw` (opt-in, off by default, needs `skipUnchangedComposition` and `skipUnchangedMeasure`) replays, on a frame that composed and measured nothing, each subtree whose draw reads, sizes, draw rects, offsets and enclosing alpha, transform and clip did not change. A subtree that moved as a whole is copied translated where that copy is bit-identical to a fresh paint, and drawn afresh where it would not be. `verifySkippedCompositions` checks a retained frame against a fresh paint, and `retainedDraws` counts the replays (#1512).
