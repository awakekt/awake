- **Compose: replay a subtree's last draw output instead of drawing it** (#1512). `ComposeHost.retainUnchangedDraw` (opt-in, with `skipUnchangedComposition` and `skipUnchangedMeasure`) replays, on a frame that composed and measured nothing, each subtree whose draw reads, sizes, draw rects, offsets from its parent and enclosing alpha, transform and clip did not change. A subtree that moved as a whole is copied translated where that copy is bit-identical to a fresh paint, and drawn afresh where it would not be; one that draws an offscreen layer or escapes its clip is never retained. `verifySkippedCompositions` compares a retained frame with a fresh paint, and `retainedDraws` counts the replays. With all three switches on, a frame of 200 moving nameplates allocates 138,402 B, down from 190,906 B (-27.5%), and a still one 426 B; the gate's new ceilings are 142,500 B and 1,000 B. Text that is still settling after a scale or a size change now keeps painting and measuring until it rests, which also fixes text staying on the distance field after a size change when `skipUnchangedMeasure` is on. Off by default: a host that leaves it off draws every node every frame as before.
