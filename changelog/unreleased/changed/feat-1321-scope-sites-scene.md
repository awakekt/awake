- **`ShaderEffectSystem` and `WorldstreamTerrainDriver` run their background work under an owner's scope.** `ShaderEffectSystem(host, assets, scope)` takes the scope its attaches run in, such as the scene session's `coroutineScope`, and parents them under a job of its own that `close()` cancels; `runProject` and `sceneSystemsFor` pass the scope the host gave `SceneHostServices.withCoroutineScope`. A `WorldstreamTerrainDriver` given no `loadScope` generates cells in a child of the scene session's scope instead of a scope of its own, and one given a `loadScope` in a child of it, so `dispose()` no longer cancels the caller's scope's other work. `ShaderEffectSystem(host, assets)` is hidden: code compiled against it still links and runs its attaches in an unowned scope of their own, as before, and code compiled now passes the scene session's scope (#1321).
