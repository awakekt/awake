- **Shader-effect textures upload to the GPU without a readback on the web.** `loadShaderEffects` decodes a `shader_effect`'s images with `decodeImage` into the new `ShaderEffectAssets.images`, and the effect's content feature binds them through the new `ContentFeature.images`. WebGPU in a browser copies them with `copyExternalImageToTexture` and draws their mips on the GPU; Vulkan and wgpu-native upload their CPU pixels as before (#1184).
  - New API, all additive: `ContentFeature.images`; `CompiledShaderDocument.contentFeature(inputs, textures, images, name)`; and `ShaderEffectAssets.images`, `imagesOf` and `hasImage`. Each comes with a constructor or overload that takes it, and the old ones are kept.
  - Behaviour change for hosts: a scene's loaded shader effects hold their images in `ShaderEffectAssets.images`, not `textures`. A host's own `textures` still draw, but an image for the same path is taken first.
