- **Behaviour change:** an analytic rounded border (`Modifier.border` on a rounded shape, or `DrawScope.drawRoundedRectBorder`) now snaps a band of 1 device pixel or more to the nearest whole pixel, half up: 1.157 draws 1, 1.5 draws 2, 2.4 draws 2. Layout already snaps the box, so a 1 dp border under a fractional canvas scale stays one solid pixel. Before, it tinted the row inside it along the whole edge, by about 16% on a thin bar at a 1.157 scale. A band under 1 device pixel, a deliberate hairline, keeps its width and partial coverage, and the corner radius stays fractional. The node-graph canvas baseline at zoom 1.5 moves: its 1 dp borders, 1.5 px there, now draw 2 px (#1545).
