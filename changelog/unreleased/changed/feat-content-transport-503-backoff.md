- **Protected content waits out a 503 with `Retry-After` like a 429, and the headless runtime path stays free of transport modules.** `TokenAuthorizedFetch` now treats a 503 Service Unavailable that carries `Retry-After` (seconds or an HTTP date) as RFC 9110 describes, and sends the request again after exactly that wait under the same `RateLimitBackoff` limits as a 429 (attempts and total wait shared with 429s; a wait past the budget fails at once with a `ContentFetchException` whose status is 503), in both `transport-ktor` and `transport-browser`. A 503 without a `Retry-After`, or with one that doesn't parse, fails the fetch at once with status 503 as before; there is no exponential fallback for it. `verifyHeadlessRuntime` now also fails when `:awake:project` or `:awake:project:runtime` reaches a `:awake:project:transport-*` module, so a played project never takes an HTTP stack the host did not choose (#1036)
