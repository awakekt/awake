- Vulkan gives each draw its material uniform slot and instance buffers by what the draw is (its mesh and material, partitioned by single, instanced, and wireframe-edge draws) instead of by its position in the frame's draw list, and `InstanceBuffer` skips rewriting a frame slot's buffer when its resident model matrices match. Culling or reordering a draw earlier in the list no longer makes every later draw rewrite its uniform slot or re-upload its instances (#1555).
