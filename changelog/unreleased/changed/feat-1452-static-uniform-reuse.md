- **A static draw's uniforms are uploaded once, not every frame (#1452).** A draw nothing animates no longer carries the frame's clock in its uniform block, so its block is the same frame after frame while the camera and light hold, and on WebGPU a draw's block, and an instanced draw's copies, are only written to the GPU when they changed. On a static scene of 1,300 draws this took the per-frame uniform upload from 666 blocks (480 KB) to none.
