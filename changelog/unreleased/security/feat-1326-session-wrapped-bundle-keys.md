- **Session-wrapped non-extractable bundle keys (`BundleKeyProvider.wrapped`).** `:awake:project` adds `BundleKeyHandle`, `BundleSessionKeyExchange`, `BundleEncryptionKey.wrapFor`, `BundleKeyStore`, and `platformBundleKeyStore` (`IndexedDbBundleKeyStore` on `wasmJs`) while keeping `BundleKeyProvider` source- and binary-compatible for SAM callers. On `wasmJs`, ephemeral RSA-2048-OAEP-SHA256 unwrapping produces a non-extractable SubtleCrypto `HKDF` `CryptoKey` (`extractable = false`) that can be structured-cloned into IndexedDB and opens sealed bundle members entirely inside SubtleCrypto without placing the master key or derived AES keys in `WebAssembly.Memory`; software-backed handles on JVM, Android, and iOS zero intermediate and master key buffers on close or authentication failure (#1326).
