# 2026-08-23: Stage 3 — shadcn straight onto `:compose:foundation`

Status: **planned, not started.** Replaces the Stage 3 bullet in
[`compose-engine/README.md`](../reference/compose-engine/README.md), which described a two-hop port
(`ui-headless` → `ui-designsystem` → samples) that this plan collapses into one.

## The change

Three layers become two. `ui-headless` is not ported — it is deleted alongside `ui-core`, and
`ui-designsystem` depends on `:awake:compose:foundation` instead.

```
before   ui-core ── ui-headless ── ui-designsystem ── samples
after    :compose:foundation ── ui-designsystem ── samples
```

**No shadcn code moves into `:awake:compose:*`.** The recipes stay in `ui-designsystem`, keep their
names and their module, and change only which module they import from. `:compose:foundation` ships
unstyled primitives — `Canvas`, `Modifier`, `Style`, `InteractionSource` — and knows nothing about
shadcn, tokens, or a theme. A `shadcnButton` in `:foundation` would be exactly the layering violation
`awake-ui-authoring` exists to prevent.

This is Compose's own split. Material3 depends directly on `androidx.compose.foundation`; there is no
headless tier between them, and no Material in foundation.

## Why `ui-headless` does not survive the move

It is not headless in the Radix sense. Measured — state markers means any of `remember`,
`WidgetState`, `mutableState`, or a `var`:

| Control | Lines | `ui-core` imports | State markers |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Buttons` | 279 | 22 | **0** |
| `Checkbox` | 225 | 23 | **0** |
| `Switch` | 193 | 22 | **0** |
| `Slider` | 167 | 19 | **0** |
| `Toggle` | 151 | 21 | **0** |
| `Surface` | 162 | 14 | **0** |

`checkbox(id, checked, …): Boolean` takes the value in and returns the next one. It holds nothing.
What those 225 lines contain is geometry and drawing — the inset square, the check mark, the label
gap — with the *colours* lifted out into a `Style` parameter.

That is rendering-without-theming, which is the opposite of Radix's behaviour-without-rendering. The
layer exists because `ui-core` gave designsystem no other way to draw a check mark without importing
`ui-core` itself.

`:compose:foundation` removes that constraint: it ships `Canvas`, `drawBehind`, `Style`,
`InteractionSource` and `Modifier`. A `shadcnCheckbox` recipe draws its own check mark directly, and
the middle hop buys nothing but an API designed twice — once headless-shaped, once shadcn-shaped.

Twelve more files (`ModifierExports`, `LayoutScopes`, `UiScope`, `Layout`, `Text`, `Canvas`, `Icon`,
`Icons`, `Surface`, `State`, `Selection`, `IntrinsicSizing`) are re-export shims that exist only to
give designsystem a `ui-core`-free import surface. `:compose:foundation` **is** that surface, so they
are pure deletion.

## What genuinely has behaviour

Six things, and they need a home in `:compose:foundation` before any recipe can be written against
them.

| From `ui-headless` | Lines | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| `TextField`, `Textarea` | 870 | **Already covered.** `BasicTextField`, caret and `EditCommand` landed in Stage 1. Selection and IME still absent |
| `ResizablePanelGroup` | 369 | Port the drag/conservation math. `ResizablePanelDragConservationTest` is its spec |
| `Dropdown`, `Menu`, `Popup` | 545 | Blocked: `07-overlay-layering` still has modal *input* capture open |
| `Slider`, `RangeSlider` | 406 | Gestures already has drag; this is the control on top of it |
| `Toast` | 79 | Blocked: the queue needs a frame clock, and `05-animation` has not started |
| `State.kt` | 87 | **Deleted.** `remember` with per-node slots replaces the string-keyed store outright |

So two of the six are blocked on Stage 1 work that was left partial. Those two gate the recipes that
need them and nothing else — the plan below orders around it rather than waiting.

## The 67 style helpers

`ui-designsystem` publishes **161** `shadcn*` functions: 94 recipes and **67 `*Style` helpers**
(`shadcnButtonStyle`, `shadcnCardStyle`, `shadcnSidebarMenuItemStyle` …).

The 2026-08 shadcn parity audit already called for collapsing the style channels into `Style` alone,
and `04-styling-theme.md` records that Compose made the same collapse. `:foundation`'s `Style` is
that shape: a function of state, composed by `styleable`.

**Fold the 67 into the recipes as the port goes**, rather than porting them and collapsing later. A
helper that exists only to hand a `Style` to the recipe two lines below it is not public API.

Whether any survive as public is a per-helper question — a caller who wants a shadcn-styled custom
widget needs *some* of them. Decide by grepping `samples/`: a helper with zero external callers goes.

## `ui-designsystem` already reaches past headless

It declares `implementation(project(":awake:ui:ui-core"))` and imports `ui.style` (20 times),
`ui.font` (7), `ui.context` (4) and `ui.theme` (3) directly — about 34 imports that bypass the
headless layer the `awake-ui-shadcn-consuming` skill says is the boundary.

Worth naming because it changes the estimate honestly: the layer being deleted was already leaking.
Those 34 imports have to be rehomed regardless of this plan.

## Order

Each step ends green. No step leaves two engines rendering the same screen.

1. **Port the four unblocked behaviours** into `:compose:foundation`: resizable panels, slider,
   range slider, and whatever of `Selection`/`ScrollState` the recipes actually need.
   `ResizablePanelDragConservationTest` and the slider tests port with them and are the gate.
2. **Finish the two blockers** — modal input capture (`07`) and the frame clock (`05`). Both are
   Stage 1 leftovers with their own value; doing them here rather than earlier is a scheduling
   choice, not a shortcut.
3. **Rewrite the 94 recipes against `:foundation`** — in place, in `ui-designsystem`. Nothing
   relocates; each recipe swaps its `ui-headless`/`ui-core` imports for `:compose:foundation` ones
   and draws what the headless control used to draw for it. Dependency order: primitives
   (`shadcnText`, `shadcnSurface`, `shadcnIcon`, `shadcnBadge`) → controls (`shadcnButton`,
   `shadcnCheckbox`, `shadcnSwitch`, `shadcnInput`) → composites (`shadcnCard`, `shadcnSidebar`,
   `shadcnTable`, `shadcnDialog`). Fold the relevant `*Style` helpers into each as it moves.
4. **Move the samples.** 1,415 `shadcn*` references across `samples/`. They move once, and mostly
   mechanically — the recipe names are unchanged by design.
5. **Delete `ui-core`, `ui-headless`, and `ui/animation`.** Together, in one commit, once nothing
   imports them. `awake/ui` keeps the primitive modules (`graphics`, `text`) and the generators
   (`heroicons`, `tailwind`, `font-atlas-generator`).

Step 4 is where [`16-migration-deltas.md`](../reference/compose-engine/16-migration-deltas.md)
earns its keep: five things change on every screen that moves, identically, and a reviewer who does
not know the list reads each one as a new bug.

## Verification

- **Pixel snapshots are the fidelity check**, per the narrowed Stage 1 gate. `UiRasterizer` is
  already `fun List<UiDrawPrimitive>.rasterize(…)` — an extension on the primitive list that knows
  nothing about either engine — and `ComposeHost.frame()` returns exactly that list. The missing
  piece is a `ComposeComponentFrame` mirroring `UiComponentFrame`'s shape.
- **State the biconditional rule before re-recording any baseline.** Every screen moves by the five
  deltas and nothing else; predict the exact moved set, then audit it. Never let re-recording be the
  fix — `awake-ui-verification`.
- **The cross-engine differ stays at five scenes** and is deleted with `ui-core`. During the port it
  is the only tool giving a field-level diagnosis when a screen moves unexpectedly.
- **Allocation ratchet holds** at 33,289 B/frame through the port. Recipes are per-frame code.

## Not in scope

- **Restyling anything.** This is a port. A recipe that looks wrong on arrival is a port bug, not a
  design opportunity — file it.
- **New controls.** The 94 that exist move; nothing is added.
- **`ui-core`'s deletion date.** It stays frozen and shipping until step 5, which is the whole reason
  the engine was built beside it rather than inside it.
- **The `Ui`-prefix rename.** Opportunistic per its own plan: rename what a module owns as it is
  touched, never introduce a new `Ui` name. A recipe being rewritten is a fine moment; a sweep is
  not this plan.
